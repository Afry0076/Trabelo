Apprenticeship · Government-funded

Level 2 Barbering Professional

A 13-month apprenticeship combining structured training with real experience in a working commercial barbershop — technical skills, professional confidence and customer service.

  • 13-month programme
  • Delivered at LTZ by qualified ATS staff
  • Funding subject to learner, employer and programme eligibility
Delivered by ATS · LTZ provides the barbershop environment and industry support
Qualification · Self-funded

Barber Diploma — NVQ Level 2 & 3

An intensive fast-track route to a recognised Level 2 or Level 3 barbering qualification, working with real clients. Some programmes can be completed in as little as three months.

  • Duration confirmed after an initial assessment
  • Separate from the funded apprenticeship route
  • Ideal for career changers and school leavers
Delivered and assessed by ATS · Practical coaching from LTZ

Our story

Talent exists everywhere. Opportunity doesn't.

Less Than Zero opened in 2017 as a single barbershop, built on one belief: people shouldn't be judged by their circumstances, qualifications, age or background — but by their potential.

Today LTZ Group is a multi-brand organisation spanning barbering, education, apprenticeships and creative enterprise. The purpose hasn't changed since day one: develop people, build brands, create legacies.

That's why training runs through everything we do — from the apprentice learning in the shop to the leadership running it.
