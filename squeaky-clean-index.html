Common questions

Before you ring

How much does a clean cost?

It depends on the size of the property and what you need doing, so every job is quoted individually rather than sold by the hour. Ring or send the form and you'll get a real figure for your property, not a guess that changes on the day.

Are you insured and DBS checked?

Yes. Every member of the team is DBS checked, inducted and given continuous improvement training, and the business is fully insured and accredited. We're happy to show the paperwork before you book.

Do I need to be in while you clean?

Not at all. Plenty of our customers are at work while we're there — that's rather the point. Key handling and alarm arrangements are agreed in writing before the first clean.

Do I get the same cleaner every time?

Wherever we possibly can, yes. We think knowing who's coming into your home matters, so we keep the same team on the same jobs unless illness or holidays force a change — and we'll tell you if that happens.

Can you handle a hoarded or badly soiled property?

Yes, and you won't be judged for it. We do this regularly, we work confidentially, and we arrive in unmarked vehicles. Ring rather than email and we'll talk it through discreetly.

Do you cover my area?

We cover Sheffield, Barnsley, Rotherham and the villages between — Stocksbridge, Deepcar, Penistone, Silkstone, Dodworth, Oughtibridge, Chapeltown, High Green, Ecclesfield, Denby Dale, Holmfirth and more. There's a checker on the home page, or just ask.
