The rules, briefly

What landlords need to know

How often do I need a gas safety check?

Every 12 months. Every gas appliance, fitting and flue you provide for tenants has to be checked annually by a Gas Safe registered engineer.

What do I do with the certificate?

Give existing tenants a copy within 28 days of the check, and give new tenants a copy before they move in. Keep your records for at least two years.

Can I renew early without losing time?

Yes. A check done up to two months before the current certificate runs out can keep the original expiry date — so there's no penalty for booking ahead.

Can you get a property ready between tenants too?

That's rather the point of us. The gas check, a boiler service, patch plastering, a coat of paint, flooring and a tidy garden — one visit, one invoice, one number to ring.
