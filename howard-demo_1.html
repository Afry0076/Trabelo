About Howard & Co

Nearly 50 years of being on your side.

Howard & Co Solicitors LLP was established in 1976 — and in those nearly five decades, we've helped thousands of families, individuals and businesses across Barnsley, Penistone and South Yorkshire navigate their most important legal moments.

We don't do big city attitudes or impersonal service. You'll deal with the same people throughout, you'll always know what's happening, and we'll always tell you the truth — even if it's not what you want to hear.

If you're unable to come to one of our offices, we're happy to come to you. That's the kind of firm we are.

Plain English

We explain things clearly — no jargon, no waffle, no confusion.

Personal

You deal with the same person throughout — not a different voice every time.

Local knowledge

Honest advice

We'll tell you where you stand — and if there's a better option, we'll say so.
