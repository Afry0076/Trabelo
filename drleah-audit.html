What's Causing the Performance Issue

The performance score of 79 is caused by a handful of specific, fixable issues.

⏱️
The page takes too long to start showing content on mobile
FCP: 2.7s · LCP: 4.6s · Speed Index: 2.7s
Key Issue

What's happening

Google measured 2.7 seconds before the first content appears on screen, and 4.6 seconds before the main content is visible on mobile. While these figures are much better than many clinics, Google's threshold for "good" is under 2.5 seconds for LCP — so there's room to improve before hitting the top band.

Why it matters to you

Patients researching cosmetic treatments on their phones in the evening are often comparing several clinics. Milliseconds matter — a site that loads slightly faster feels more polished and professional. With everything else at 100, it's a shame performance is the one thing bringing the overall score down.

What the rebuild fixes

A clean, purpose-built site without the overhead of a Wix website builder would score 97+ on performance — loading well under 1.5 seconds on mobile. Everything renders instantly, first impressions are excellent, and nothing slows the experience down.

📦
Unused JavaScript is adding unnecessary download weight
Est. savings: 170 KB of unused JS
Notable

What's happening

Your site is loading 170KB of JavaScript that it doesn't actually use. This is typical of website builders like Wix — they load a large amount of code to power the builder itself and all possible features, even the ones your site isn't using.

Why it matters to you

That extra 170KB has to be downloaded by every single visitor before the page can fully load. On a slow mobile connection it adds time — time that a potential patient is sitting looking at a loading screen rather than reading about your treatments.

What the rebuild fixes

A custom-built site carries only the code it genuinely needs. No website builder overhead, no unused scripts. The result is a leaner, faster site that doesn't make visitors wait for things they'll never see.

🖼️
Images and caching have minor optimisation opportunities
Image savings: 12 KB · Cache savings: 12 KB · Duplicate JS: 18 KB
Minor

What's happening

Small amounts of image data could be further compressed, caching isn't quite set up optimally for returning visitors, and there's a small amount of duplicated JavaScript loading. None of these are major issues individually, but they add up to a slightly slower experience than is possible.

Why it matters to you

For a clinic where first impressions matter — and where patients may visit the site multiple times before booking — every small improvement to load time contributes to a smoother, more professional experience. These are the kinds of polish details that separate a good site from a great one.

What the rebuild fixes

Proper image compression, correct caching headers and zero duplicated code from day one. All small wins individually — but together they push the performance score from 79 to 97+, and the difference is genuinely noticeable on a phone.

🔧
The site is built on Wix — which limits what's possible
Wix builder overhead · Limited control over performance · Hard to optimise
Worth Knowing

What's happening

The site is built on Wix, which is a popular website builder. The performance issues above are largely a consequence of that — Wix adds its own code, scripts and infrastructure that you can't fully control or remove, which always puts a ceiling on how fast the site can load.

Why it matters to you

It means the performance score of 79 is probably close to the best that's achievable on the current platform, regardless of what other tweaks are made. To genuinely push into the 90+ range — where your accessibility and SEO already sit — the site needs to be built on a platform with full control over the code.

What the rebuild fixes

A custom-coded site has no platform overhead at all. Every line of code is there for a reason. That's what allows the performance score to reach 97+ — something Wix-based sites simply can't achieve regardless of how well optimised the content is.

It keeps everything that's already working — and adds the things the current site can't do.
