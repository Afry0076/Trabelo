Good to know

Before your appointment

❥
Patch test Required at least 24 hours before any tint or lash treatment, and it only takes a couple of minutes. First-time clients especially — I'll arrange it when you book.
Come with clean lashes No mascara, eye make-up or oil-based products on the day. It genuinely makes a difference to how well the lift takes.
Aftercare Keep brows and lashes dry for 24 hours afterwards, and avoid steam or heavy heat. I'll talk you through it all before you leave.

Booking policy

Securing your appointment

To secure your appointment, a 50% non-refundable deposit is required at the time of booking.

  • Your deposit is deducted from the total cost of your treatment.
  • If you arrive 15 minutes or more late, your appointment may need to be rescheduled and the deposit will be lost.
  • Cancelling or rescheduling within 24 hours of your appointment makes the deposit non-refundable.
  • Failing to attend without letting me know means the deposit is forfeited.
I completely understand that emergencies and unexpected situations happen. But as a self-employed beauty therapist, last-minute cancellations and missed appointments mean I lose booking time I often can't fill at short notice — and this is my only source of income. Thank you so much for supporting my small business. I can't wait to see you at your appointment! ❥
