What's Going Wrong

Here's a plain-English breakdown of the main issues and what they mean for your clinic.

🐢
The site loads too slowly on mobile
Performance score: 58/100 · Largest Contentful Paint: 15.4 seconds
Critical

What's happening

Google measured the main content on your page taking 15.4 seconds to fully appear on a mobile phone. The overall performance score is 58/100 — anything below 90 is flagged as a problem. The biggest culprits are oversized images, scripts that block the page from loading, and excess unused code.

Why it matters to you

Most people searching for a cosmetic clinic are browsing on their phone, often in the evening. Research shows that if a site takes longer than 3 seconds to load, over half of visitors will just leave. That's potential clients for your Botox, fillers or Morpheus8 treatments clicking away to a competitor before they've even seen what you offer.

What the rebuild fixes

A rebuilt site would score 97+ on performance. Images would be properly compressed and sized. Unnecessary scripts would be removed. The result is a page that loads in under 1.5 seconds on mobile — the kind of speed that keeps people on the page long enough to book.

🖼️
Images are far too large and unoptimised
Estimated savings: 704 KB · Images missing size attributes
Critical

What's happening

The images on your site haven't been resized or compressed for the web. Google flagged 704KB of unnecessary image data being loaded — over half a megabyte of extra download your visitors have to wait for, every time they open the page.

Why it matters to you

For a cosmetic clinic, before-and-after photos and treatment imagery are incredibly important — they're often what persuades someone to book. But if those images are making the site sluggish, they're doing more harm than good. People won't wait. They'll go elsewhere.

What the rebuild fixes

Every image on the new site would be properly compressed and served in modern formats like WebP, which are much smaller without any visible quality loss. Result and gallery imagery would still look stunning — it'll just load instantly instead of making people wait.

⛔
Scripts are blocking the page from loading
Render-blocking requests: estimated 1,940ms delay
Critical

What's happening

There are scripts (code files) on your site that have to fully load before the page can even start showing anything to the visitor. This is adding nearly 2 full seconds to your load time before the page even begins appearing — and that's on top of everything else slowing it down.

Why it matters to you

Those 2 seconds of blank screen feel much longer when you're on a phone. It's the digital equivalent of someone walking through the front door of your clinic and being made to wait in an empty room before anyone acknowledges them. First impressions matter — online as much as in person.

What the rebuild fixes

A clean, purpose-built site carries no unnecessary third-party scripts. The ones that are genuinely needed would be loaded in a way that doesn't block the page from appearing first. Visitors see something the moment they land.

♿
Accessibility issues — some visitors struggle to use the site
Accessibility score: 70/100 · Missing alt text, contrast issues, broken lists
Important

What's happening

Several images on your site have no descriptions (called "alt text"), meaning screen readers — used by visually impaired visitors — can't interpret them. There are also colour contrast issues where text is hard to read, and broken list structures that confuse assistive technology.

Why it matters to you

Accessibility issues aren't just about complying with guidelines — they affect real visitors. An older client who uses larger text or a screen reader, or someone with visual difficulties, simply won't be able to use parts of your site. That's a client lost before they even get in touch. Google also uses accessibility as a ranking signal.

What the rebuild fixes

The new site would score 98+ on accessibility — proper alt text on every image, sufficient colour contrast throughout, correctly structured page elements, and touch targets sized correctly for fingers on a phone screen.

🔍
SEO issues are limiting your Google visibility
SEO score: 92/100 · Missing image alt text · Pinch-zoom disabled
Important

What's happening

Your site prevents users from pinching to zoom in on mobile — which Google flags as a usability issue and penalises in search rankings. Missing image alt text also means Google can't "read" your images, which limits how well they rank in image search. These aren't massive problems individually, but they add up.

Why it matters to you

SEO (search engine optimisation) is what determines whether your clinic appears near the top of Google when someone in Doncaster searches "lip filler near me" or "botox Doncaster." Every small issue chips away at that ranking. When competitors' sites score higher, they appear above you — and most people don't scroll past the top few results.

What the rebuild fixes

A rebuilt site would score 100 on SEO — proper image descriptions, zoom enabled on mobile, clean page structure, fast loading (which Google rewards heavily), and correct heading hierarchy throughout. Everything set up the right way from the start.

💳
Caching isn't set up properly — every visit reloads everything
Use efficient cache lifetimes: estimated savings of 396 KB per visit
Notable

What's happening

Caching is a way of telling a visitor's browser "remember this file — you don't need to download it again next time." Your site isn't doing this properly, which means returning visitors have to download everything from scratch every single time they come back.

Why it matters to you

Someone who's looked at your site once and comes back to check prices or book an appointment will have to wait for the whole thing to load again from the beginning. That's a poor experience for a returning client and contributes to the overall slowness that's hurting your scores.

What the rebuild fixes

Proper caching would be configured from day one. Returning visitors see the site load almost instantly, because their browser already has most of it saved. It makes a noticeable difference to the experience, especially on slower mobile connections.
