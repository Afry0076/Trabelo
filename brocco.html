Our story

About Brocco on the Park

Brocco is very much in the heart of its community — independently owned and run, with a flock of accolades, reviews and awards, local, regional and national, for both the hotel and the restaurant.

Our Edwardian villa by the park was taken from a derelict state in 2014 with the view to creating an experience that was affordably stylish. Whether you want to pop in for a cup of freshly brewed coffee and a chat, or a weekend away with a loved one, there is an experience for everyone.

Our mantra is eat well, sleep well, live well — and everything at Brocco has been thought about. If it can be home-made, hand-picked or personalised, it has been. Nothing is too much trouble when it comes to making you feel at home.

The building was designed in keeping with its Edwardian heritage and internally styled with simplicity and cosiness in mind, resulting in clean, uncluttered lines and a soothing palette of colours.
