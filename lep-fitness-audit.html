What's Not Working — and Why It Matters

Four specific issues found on your site, in plain terms.

Critical — Performance

Performance score of 30 — your page takes nearly 25 seconds to load on mobile

What's happening

Google's mobile test shows the Largest Contentful Paint is 24.7 seconds — the main content of your page takes nearly 25 seconds to appear on a phone. The first thing to show takes 17.5 seconds. There are 2,114KB of unused JavaScript — over 2 megabytes of code downloaded and thrown away on every visit. Total page size is 7.75 megabytes. There are 20 long blocking tasks and render-blocking requests with 1,550ms of potential savings.

What this means for your clients

Someone Googling "personal trainer Sheffield" at 8pm on their phone is waiting 25 seconds to read your first word. Your transformations, your testimonials, Nick's story — none of it gets a look in. Over half of mobile users leave in under 3 seconds. People who would be your best clients are clicking away before they've seen a single word.

The fix

A hand-built site with no WordPress plugin bloat, only the code that's needed, images properly compressed, and no render-blocking scripts. Target performance score: 97+. Load time: under 2 seconds.

Accessibility — Score 72

Zoom is blocked — users can't pinch to zoom on mobile

What's happening

Your site uses maximum-scale=1 in the viewport tag, which disables pinch-to-zoom on mobile. This is a specific accessibility violation. Google also found form fields without labels, iframes without titles, links without discernible names, ARIA attribute errors, and colour contrast issues. These issues combine to bring your accessibility score to 72.

What this means for your clients

Any client who needs to zoom in to read your testimonials or package details on their phone simply can't. For a studio that helps people rebuild confidence and fitness at all ages and stages — the site should be accessible to everyone who might benefit from it.

The fix

Zoom re-enabled, all form fields and iframes properly labelled, full contrast compliance, ARIA corrected. Accessibility target: 98+.

Best Practices — Score 73

Browser errors, third-party cookies and missing security headers

What's happening

Google found 5 third-party cookies being set on every visit (flagged as a trust and privacy issue), browser console errors being logged (JavaScript running but failing), and missing security headers including Content Security Policy, HSTS, COOP and XFO. These explain the best practices score of 73.

What this means for your clients

Browser errors slow the page further and can break elements. Missing security headers leave the site technically exposed. Browsers flag these in their developer tools — and increasingly in warnings shown to users.

The fix

A clean codebase with no runtime errors, no unnecessary third-party cookies, and proper security headers set. Best Practices target: 100.

SEO — Score 85

Invalid robots.txt — Google may not be crawling all your pages

What's happening

Google found an error in your robots.txt file. The robots.txt is a file that tells search engine crawlers which pages they're allowed to visit. An error in it means Google may be misreading the instructions — potentially blocking itself from pages you want indexed, or indexing pages you don't. Google also found 1 link without descriptive text.

What this means for your clients

You've put years of work into transformation content, testimonials and service pages. If the robots.txt is misconfigured, some of that content might be invisible to Google — people searching for those terms never find the pages you've built. It's like having a locked door on some of your rooms.

The fix

A correctly configured robots.txt, all links properly labelled, full SEO structure. SEO target: 100.
