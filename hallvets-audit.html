What's Going Wrong

Here's what Google's tools found — explained in plain English, with what it means for your clients and your practice.

🔒
Critical · Security
The site is loading some content insecurely

What's happening

Google found 2 insecure requests — content being loaded over HTTP rather than HTTPS on what should be a secure site. In plain English: some bits of your website are being served without encryption. Modern browsers (Chrome, Safari, Firefox) flag this and may show a warning to visitors, or block the content entirely.

What this means for your clients

This is why your best practices score is 46 — the second-lowest score I've seen of any practice in South Yorkshire. Clients visiting on a modern browser might see a security warning in their address bar, or content might not load at all. For a veterinary practice where trust is everything, that's not a good first impression.

The fix

On a rebuilt site, everything is served over HTTPS from the start. No mixed content, no browser warnings, no security flags from Google.

🐌
Critical · Performance
Main content takes nearly 6 seconds to appear on mobile

What's happening

Google measured what's called "Largest Contentful Paint" — the time until the main visible content of your homepage has loaded. For your site, that's 5.9 seconds on a typical mobile connection. The benchmark Google considers acceptable is 2.5 seconds. Your site also has a layout shift score of 0.324 — well above the 0.1 threshold — which means the page visibly jumps around as it loads.

What this means for your clients

Most people will close a website that doesn't load within 3–4 seconds. At nearly 6 seconds, you're losing a significant number of potential new clients before they've even seen your services. People searching for a vet in Dinnington or Handsworth will often visit 2 or 3 practices' websites — if yours is the slow one, they move on. It also affects your Google ranking: Google now uses real-world load speed as a ranking factor.

The fix

A bespoke rebuild loads only what it needs. Images are properly compressed and sized. No WordPress theme overhead, no WPBakery plugin loading. A well-built site typically hits 2–3 seconds even with good imagery.

📦
Performance · Page Size
The site is downloading 8.7 MB of data — most of it unnecessary

What's happening

Your total page size is 8,714 KB — nearly 9 megabytes. Google identified 6,298 KB of that could be saved just by improving image delivery (resizing, compressing and serving modern formats). On top of that, there's 393 KB of unused JavaScript and 135 KB of unused CSS loading on every page — code the site downloads but never actually uses.

What this means for your clients

Every extra megabyte means more waiting. On a standard mobile connection, 9 MB takes time. Clients on slower rural connections — of which there are plenty in Dinnington and the surrounding area — will feel this more than most. It's also using more of their mobile data allowance than it should.

The fix

A properly built site typically loads in under 1 MB. Images are compressed and served in modern formats. There's no platform bloat — just the code the site actually needs.

📐
Performance · User Experience
The page jumps around as it loads

What's happening

Your Cumulative Layout Shift score is 0.324. This measures how much the page visually jumps around as images and content load in. Google's threshold for a good experience is below 0.1. At 0.324, you're more than three times over that. The main cause is images loading without defined dimensions, so the browser doesn't know how much space to leave for them.

What this means for your clients

If someone is trying to tap a phone number or a menu link and the page suddenly shifts under their finger, they end up tapping the wrong thing — or give up. It's a genuinely frustrating experience on mobile, and it directly affects how Google scores your site.

The fix

All images on a rebuilt site are given explicit dimensions. Content loads in place with no jumping. Layout shift score on a well-built site is typically 0.0–0.05.

🔍
SEO · Visibility
No meta description — Google can't describe your practice properly

What's happening

Your site is missing a meta description — the short paragraph of text that appears under your link in Google search results. When this is missing, Google either shows nothing at all or pulls a random line from the page, which is rarely the most compelling thing it could say about you.

What this means for your clients

Your SEO score of 92 means you're showing up in Google searches — but your listing doesn't say much about who you are. A potential new client Googling "vets Dinnington" or "vets Handsworth" might see your link with a vague or blank description, and click a competitor instead. It's a small thing that makes a real difference to click-through rates.

The fix

A rebuilt site includes carefully written descriptions for every page — tuned to what people are actually searching for in your area.

🏗️
Platform Limitation
Built on WordPress + WPBakery — a platform with real ceilings

What's happening

Your site runs on WordPress using WPBakery Page Builder — a drag-and-drop page builder. It's a common setup, but WPBakery in particular is known for generating bloated, heavy HTML and loading a lot of JavaScript that slows things down. WordPress itself is a general-purpose CMS that loads a lot of code regardless of what your specific site needs.

What this means for you

Some of the performance issues above are direct consequences of the platform — not things that can be fully fixed without changing it. Even with careful optimisation on WordPress, there's a ceiling on how fast it can go. And WPBakery sites are notoriously difficult to maintain as the platform ages.

The fix

A clean bespoke build — lightweight, no platform overhead, hosted on modern infrastructure. The finished site is yours to own outright, no monthly WordPress licence or plugin fees.
