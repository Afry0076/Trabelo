What We Do

Full Veterinary Care for Every Pet

From routine check-ups to emergency care — Green Vets is your local independent practice, providing excellent healthcare to every beloved companion.

🩺

General Consultations

Health checks, vaccinations, microchipping and routine treatments. Book an appointment online or by phone — open Monday to Saturday.

🚨

Emergency Care — 24/7

We're always available for emergencies. Call 0114 264 5454 any time, day or night, and we'll make sure your pet is seen as quickly as possible.

🔬

Diagnostics & Testing

On-site diagnostic capabilities to get you answers quickly. Blood tests, urine analysis and more — helping us understand your pet's health without unnecessary delays.

💊

Preventative Care

Flea and worm treatments, parasite prevention, seasonal health advice. We believe preventing illness is just as important as treating it.

✂️

Surgery & Procedures

Routine and non-routine surgical procedures carried out on site with full anaesthetic monitoring. Your pet's safety and comfort are our absolute priority.

🐴

Large Animal Practice

We also run a dedicated large animal and equine veterinary practice. Visit greenvetsequine.co.uk for full details on our equine services.

Our Services

Caring for Pets of All Shapes and Sizes

Green Vets is a full-service independent veterinary practice. Whatever your pet needs — routine, emergency, surgical, or preventative — we're here for it.

🩺

General Consultations

Routine appointments for all small animals — dogs, cats, rabbits, guinea pigs and more. Health checks, vaccinations, weight monitoring and general health advice.

🚨

24/7 Emergency Care

Available around the clock for genuine emergencies. Call 0114 264 5454 any time — our team will advise you on what to do and see you as quickly as possible.

💉

Vaccinations

Annual boosters and puppy/kitten primary courses for dogs, cats and rabbits. We'll remind you when your pet is due and make the process as straightforward as possible.

🔬

In-House Diagnostics

Blood tests, urine analysis and other diagnostics carried out on site. Faster results mean faster treatment — no waiting days for lab results for urgent cases.

✂️

Surgery

Routine procedures (neutering, dental work, lump removal) and non-routine surgeries when needed. Full anaesthetic monitoring throughout every procedure.

💊

Flea & Parasite Control

Prescription-strength flea and worm treatments, regular parasite prevention programmes, and seasonal advice on keeping your pet protected year-round.

📋

Health Plans & Schemes

We offer health and wellness programmes to help you spread the cost of routine care and make sure your pet stays on top of their vaccinations and treatments.

🐾

Microchipping

Quick, safe microchipping for dogs, cats and rabbits. We'll register the chip in the national database so your pet can be reunited with you if they're ever lost.

🐴

Large Animal & Equine

Separate dedicated equine practice at Green Vets Equine. Full large animal veterinary services — visit greenvetsequine.co.uk for details.

Who We Are

One of the Few Truly Independent Practices Left in South Yorkshire

At Green Vets, we pride ourselves on being one of the rare independently run veterinary practices remaining in the South Yorkshire area. We haven't been bought by a corporate group. We haven't been standardised. We're still run by people who genuinely care.

Our mission is simple: to deliver excellent healthcare to your beloved companions in a fast and friendly manner. Whether it's a routine check-up, an emergency at 3am, or a difficult diagnosis — you'll always get real people who know your pet and care about getting it right.
