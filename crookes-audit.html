Google's testing tool found over half a megabyte of code on your site that isn't actually doing anything — it's just there because GoDaddy loads it on every page automatically. That's what's making the site slow. Even on a fast home broadband connection, people are waiting 2–3 seconds before they can do anything on the page. The rebuilt site loads only what's needed — so it's fast everywhere.

The site has some technical issues that are quietly hurting your Google ranking
There are a few things on the current site that Google's testing tool flags up — buttons without labels, images without descriptions, text that's hard to read. Most of these are invisible to a normal visitor but Google sees them, and they affect where the site appears in search results. It also means people who use screen readers (visually impaired visitors) can't properly use the site. All of these are fixed in the rebuild.
