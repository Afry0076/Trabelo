Welcome

The Heart of Italian Dining on Ecclesall Road

Since opening our doors in 1996, Nonna's has become a beloved institution in Sheffield — bringing the warmth, passion and flavours of Tuscany to the heart of Ecclesall Road. Whether you're here for a long lazy lunch, a romantic dinner, or a celebration with friends, our menu is crafted with the finest ingredients and an unwavering love for authentic Italian cuisine.

As the evening unfolds, Nonna's transforms — resident DJs, cocktails, and the spirit of La Dolce Vita. More than dinner; an experience.

Our Story

From Tuscany to Ecclesall Road,
with Love

A Family Legacy

For Maurizio Mori, food has always been about family, tradition, and heart. As the son of Moreno Mori — an influential figure who helped shape Sheffield's Italian dining scene in the 1970s and '80s — Maurizio grew up surrounded by the warmth of the kitchen and the rich, simple flavours of Tuscany.

Inspired by his father's legacy, Maurizio set out to do something of his own: a place where traditional Tuscan recipes meet the soul of Nonna's cooking. Every dish carries a sense of home, crafted with love, care, and just a little twist to make it special.

That passion extends to every corner of the kitchen. Head Chef Ross Sayles has been at the pass for over 20 years — making him as much a part of Nonna's as the recipes themselves. It's something our customers truly feel, and we're proud of the team who bring that vision to life every single day.
