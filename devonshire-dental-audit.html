What's Going Wrong

Critical
Your main content takes 22.6 seconds to load on mobile
Largest Contentful Paint of 22.6s — this is the longest I've seen on any Sheffield dental practice I've audited.
Why it matters to you

Most people looking for a dentist in Sheffield city centre are searching on their phone during a lunch break or an evening. At 22.6 seconds, your site takes so long to show its main content that the vast majority of visitors will have already left and found a competitor before they've even seen your name. Google's own research puts the typical abandonment threshold at around 3 seconds.

What the fix looks like

The main culprit is your images — Google identified over 3.4MB of image savings possible, which is enormous. On a rebuilt site, images are properly compressed, converted to modern formats, and sized for the screen they're being viewed on. Combined with removing the render-blocking code, load time drops dramatically. The target is 97+ on performance.

Critical
Over 3.4MB of unnecessary image data being sent to every visitor
Google flagged 3,492 KB of image savings — that's 3.4MB of data that doesn't need to be there.
Why it matters to you

Your team photos are a great asset — patients want to see who they're going to meet. But right now those images are being loaded at full resolution every time, on every device. Someone on a mobile phone is downloading the same image as someone on a desktop with a fast connection, even though they only need a fraction of the size.

What the fix looks like

On a rebuilt site every image is compressed, converted to WebP format (which is 30–50% smaller than JPG with no visible quality loss), and served at the right resolution for the device. Your team photos stay — they just load in a fraction of the time.

Critical
Render-blocking code is adding 2.3 seconds before anything appears
Estimated savings of 2,310ms — nearly 2.5 seconds of unnecessary delay before the page starts loading visually.
Why it matters to you

Your site is built on WordPress with Elementor, which loads a large amount of CSS and JavaScript before it shows anything on screen. It's like having to read the entire ingredients list before you can open a tin — the browser is waiting for code to finish loading before it can show the patient anything at all.

What the fix looks like

A hand-coded rebuild doesn't carry any of that overhead. The visual content loads first, and any background scripts load after. It's one of the biggest single wins available to a site in your position.

Critical
Total page size of nearly 5MB is far too large
Google flagged a total network payload of 4,912 KB — a well-optimised site should be under 1MB.
Why it matters to you

Every time someone visits your site, their phone has to download almost 5MB of data. On a typical 4G connection that takes a long time. It's also worth knowing that patients on limited data plans may get charged more just for visiting your site — not a great first impression.

What the fix looks like

A rebuilt site typically comes in under 500KB total — less than a tenth of your current size. This is achieved through image compression, removing unused code, and building lean from the ground up rather than through a page builder.

Moderate
A few accessibility issues that affect some patients
Score of 91/100 — colour contrast, heading order, and some links without descriptive text.
Why it matters to you

You work hard to make the practice welcoming for patients who find dental visits difficult. It's worth making sure the website reflects the same care — particularly for patients who use screen readers or who zoom in on their phones. Some of your text doesn't meet the minimum contrast requirements, and some links don't have descriptive enough text for assistive technology.

What the fix looks like

A rebuild targets 98 on accessibility — proper contrast, correct heading structure, and fully descriptive link text throughout.
