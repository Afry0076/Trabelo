What's Not Working — and Why It Matters

Three specific issues, in plain terms.

Performance

Render-blocking scripts — 1,600ms delay before the page can start loading

What's happening

Google found render-blocking requests with 1,600ms of potential savings. These are scripts loading in the page header that force the browser to stop and wait before it can show anything. It also found 124KB of image savings — images that could be smaller and load faster without any visible quality loss.

What this means for your clients

Your page does load faster than most — but shaving 1.6 seconds off the initial delay and optimising images would push your performance score from 87 to comfortably over 97. That's the difference between a site that loads "pretty quickly" and one that loads before most people notice.

The fix

All scripts loaded non-blocking, images compressed and properly sized. Target performance score: 97+.

Accessibility

Colour contrast, heading order and SVG icons without text alternatives

What's happening

Google found that some colour combinations don't meet the minimum contrast ratio — text against its background is slightly harder to read for some users. It also found heading elements not in descending order (which affects how screen readers and Google navigate your page) and SVG icons used as images without alt text — icons that assistive technology can't describe.

What this means for your clients

These are small gaps, but a 94 on accessibility means some visitors have a slightly harder experience. A 98+ means everyone can read, navigate and understand the site regardless of how they're accessing it.

The fix

Full contrast compliance, correct heading hierarchy, all SVGs labelled. Accessibility target: 98+.

SEO

2 links without descriptive text and browser console errors

What's happening

Google found 2 links without descriptive text — likely image or icon links where the label Google uses to understand the link is missing. It also found browser errors being logged, which suggests some JavaScript on the page is failing silently.

What this means for your clients

Missing link labels mean Google can't fully read how your pages connect. Console errors mean code is failing — usually harmlessly, but occasionally causing small visual or functional issues. Both are worth cleaning up to hit 100 on SEO and 100 on best practices.

The fix

All links properly labelled, errors resolved, clean SEO structure throughout. SEO target: 100. Best Practices target: 100.
