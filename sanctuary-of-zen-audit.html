Hi Olivia — I'm Aidan. I do web design across Sheffield and the surrounding area, and I came across Sanctuary of Zen while looking at local wellness businesses. Genuinely lovely reviews, and exactly the kind of personal, caring service that deserves a website that reflects it.

Right now, there isn't really a "website" as such — just a booking page, and I ran that through Google's testing tool. It scored 35 out of 100 for performance, with the main content taking nearly 18 seconds to appear on a phone. For a wellness brand built around calm, that's a pretty ironic first impression. Here's the full picture.

What's Not Working — and Why It Matters

This is your booking page, built on Acuity Scheduling — so none of this is a reflection of you or your work, it's the platform. Here's what's actually happening.

Critical

Performance score of 35 — the main content takes nearly 18 seconds to appear on mobile

What's happening

Google's mobile test shows the Largest Contentful Paint is 17.9 seconds — that's how long it takes for the main content to actually appear on a phone. The first thing to appear takes 9 seconds on its own. The page is also blocked from rendering for 8.2 seconds by scripts and styles that have to load first.

What this means for your clients

Someone finds you on Facebook, taps through on their phone to check your prices or book a massage — and waits the best part of 18 seconds before anything useful shows up. Most people give up within 3 seconds. For a business built on trust and a sense of calm, a slow, janky booking page undoes exactly the impression you're trying to give before anyone's even read a word.

The fix

A proper website that loads fast and shows off your treatments, reviews and story straight away — with your existing booking system linked in, not replaced, so nothing about how you take bookings has to change.

Critical

The page loads 4.2MB of data — most of it never gets used

What's happening

Google found 1,089 KB of unused JavaScript and 40 KB of unused CSS loading on every visit, plus 688 KB of images that could be delivered far more efficiently. In total the page is 4,219 KB — for a page that's really just a list of treatments and a booking calendar.

What this means for your clients

All of that has to be downloaded before someone can even see your prices — especially painful for anyone checking on mobile data rather than home wifi. It's also most of the reason the page takes so long to load in the first place.

The fix

A hand-built site loads only what it actually needs — no bloated booking-platform overhead. Target load size: a fraction of the current 4.2MB.

Layout

The page visibly jumps around as it loads

What's happening

Google measures this as Cumulative Layout Shift of 0.195 — content is moving around on screen as the page loads, mostly because images don't have their size set in advance, so the page doesn't know how much space to leave for them.

What this means for your clients

Buttons and prices shift position while someone's trying to tap them — easy to end up tapping the wrong thing, or just feeling like the page is a bit unstable. Small thing, but it chips away at the "calm and cared-for" feeling the rest of your brand works hard for.

The fix

Properly sized images and a stable layout from the first paint — nothing jumping around. Target CLS: under 0.1, comfortably in Google's "good" range.

Trust

There's nowhere for a new client to see your reviews, your story, or what you actually offer

What's happening

The booking page is just a list of treatment names and prices — no photos, no reviews, nothing about you or your qualifications. Your genuinely excellent reviews only live on Facebook, where someone has to scroll to find them.

What this means for your clients

Booking a massage or Reiki session is personal — people want to feel comfortable with who they're booking before they commit, especially first-time clients. Right now, nothing on the booking page itself builds that trust; they have to already believe in you before they even land there.

The fix

Your reviews, your qualifications and your story front and centre — so trust is built before someone even reaches the booking calendar, not left to chance.
