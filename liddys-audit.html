What's going wrong

Six things worth knowing about.

Some of these are technical, some are about how the site presents the firm. All of them are fixable — and none of them are your fault.

🐌
The site takes 14 seconds to fully load on a phone — and someone injured or stressed won't wait that long
Performance 55 · LCP 14.2s · Speed Index 6.1s
Think about who's searching for a personal injury solicitor. It's usually someone who's just been in an accident, or who's been dealing with a difficult situation and has finally decided to look into making a claim. They're searching on their phone, probably not in a great place, and they need the page to load quickly and clearly tell them what to do. At 14 seconds, most people have already pressed back and moved on to the next result. Google's own data shows most mobile users abandon a page after just a few seconds. A performance score of 55 is firmly in the red.
✓ Fixed in the rebuildNo WordPress bloat, no render-blocking scripts. The rebuilt site loads in under a second on mobile. Performance 97+.
🗑️
335KB of JavaScript is loading on every page visit — and the site doesn't use any of it
335KB unused JS identified by Google · render-blocking requests adding 1,110ms delay
Google PageSpeed flagged 335KB of JavaScript that loads every time someone visits the site but isn't actually used by any page. On top of that, there are render-blocking requests that are adding over a second of delay before anything even appears on screen — meaning a visitor is staring at a blank page for a second before the site starts to show. This is very common with WordPress sites using third-party plugins and themes. The code gets added over time and no one removes it. The only proper fix is to build something that only loads what it needs.
✓ Fixed in the rebuildZero unused JavaScript. Zero render-blocking requests. The page starts rendering immediately.
♿
Five accessibility issues — including low contrast text and buttons without names
Accessibility 83 · buttons without names · links without names · low contrast · heading order
Google flagged five specific accessibility problems on the current site: buttons that don't have an accessible name (so someone using a screen reader can't tell what the button does), links with no discernible name, iframes without titles, text where the foreground and background colours don't have enough contrast to be easily readable, and headings that skip levels in a way that confuses assistive technology. An accessibility score of 83 is the lowest of any of the scores — and for a firm that handles cases for people who've been hurt or who may be vulnerable, this matters both practically and reputationally.
✓ Fixed in the rebuildAll buttons and links properly named. Contrast ratios meet WCAG 2.1 AA throughout. Heading structure logical and sequential. Accessibility 98.
🔍
The site's SEO score is 92 — not 100 — because of links with no descriptive text
SEO 92 · 3 links without descriptive text identified · console errors logged
Google found three links on the site with no descriptive text — things like "click here" or "read more" rather than something that tells Google (and the visitor) where the link goes. Google uses link text as a signal when deciding how to rank pages for searches like "no win no fee solicitors Barnsley" or "clinical negligence claim Wakefield". Every point matters when you're competing with other local firms for those searches. There are also browser console errors logged — small technical issues that can affect how the page loads and how Google interprets it.
✓ Fixed in the rebuildAll links have clear, descriptive text. Zero console errors. SEO 100.
⭐
Your 5-star rating and 99% recommendation stat aren't visible enough — they should be the first things someone sees
Key trust signals buried · not prominent on mobile · hero doesn't lead with credentials
Liddy's has genuinely strong independent credentials — a 5-star rating and 99% of clients saying they'd recommend you. These are the kinds of numbers that make someone choose you over the next firm on the list. But on the current site they're displayed in a relatively small line of text that gets lost in the page. On mobile, a visitor scrolling quickly might not even notice them. The hero section — the first thing anyone sees — currently leads with three generic "Start Now" buttons rather than the firm's strongest selling point, which is that track record.
✓ Fixed in the rebuildThe 5-star rating and 99% recommendation stat are the headline of the hero section on every device. They also appear in the topbar, trust strip, sidebar card and footer — impossible to miss.
🤖
AI tools and voice assistants can't fully navigate the site yet
Agentic Browsing 2/3 — one check failing
When someone asks Google's AI Overview or a voice assistant "best no win no fee solicitors near Barnsley" or "clinical negligence solicitor Wakefield", those tools need to be able to properly read and navigate your site to include it in their answer. The current site scores 2 out of 3 on Google's Agentic Browsing test — there's one element blocking it from being fully usable by AI agents. As more people search this way, being in that answer becomes increasingly valuable for a firm like Liddy's.
