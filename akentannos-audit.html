Your one real issue is performance — specifically, how long your site takes to load on a mobile phone. And for a restaurant on Sharrow Vale Road where someone's deciding where to go for dinner tonight, that matters more than you might think.

The main issue

Your site takes over 6 seconds to load on mobile

Google measures something called the Largest Contentful Paint — basically, how long before someone on a mobile phone actually sees your main content appear on screen. For your site, that's 6.2 seconds.

What does 6.2 seconds actually mean?

Google's threshold for a "good" LCP is 2.5 seconds. Anything over 4 seconds is considered poor. Your site takes 6.2 seconds — more than twice Google's recommended maximum. On a slow 4G connection, which is what the test uses (the same speed most people are on when searching on their phones while out), someone is looking at a blank or mostly-empty screen for over six seconds before they can actually see your restaurant.

Research consistently shows that more than half of mobile users will abandon a page if it takes longer than 3 seconds to load. By 6 seconds, the majority are already gone.

One piece of good news: your site has zero blocking time (TBT: 0ms), which means once the page does load, it's immediately interactive and responsive. The problem is getting there in the first place.

Why it's slow

The Wix platform is doing it

Your website is built on Wix, which is a very capable tool for building a site — but it comes with a lot of behind-the-scenes code that runs whether you need it or not. That code has to be downloaded by every visitor before your pages can load. Here's what the audit found:

139 KiB unused JavaScript 36 KiB duplicate JavaScript 12 KiB legacy JavaScript 37 KiB poor cache efficiency 416 internal tracking measurements 5+ preconnect connections
  • ⚠️
    139 KiB of unused JavaScript. Your site is loading code that it never actually runs — code for features you might not even use. That's 139 kilobytes every visitor has to download before anything can load. On mobile, that takes time.
  • ⚠️
    Render-blocking scripts. Some scripts are being loaded before your page content, which forces the browser to stop and wait rather than loading your restaurant in front of people. This is a common Wix problem — the platform loads its own tools in a way that delays your content.
  • ⚠️
    Duplicate and legacy JavaScript. There's code being loaded twice (36 KiB of duplicates), and some older JavaScript that modern browsers no longer need (12 KiB). Neither of these are things you can fix inside Wix — they're baked into how the platform works.
  • ⚠️
    Poor cache lifetimes. When someone visits your site, their phone should save a copy so the next visit is faster. Your site's caching isn't set up well — so every visit takes the same amount of time to load, even for returning customers. Estimated saving: 37 KiB per visit.
  • ⚠️
    416 internal performance measurements. Wix runs 416 internal timing and tracking measurements behind the scenes on every page load. This is Wix monitoring its own platform — useful for them, but it adds overhead to every single visit to your website.

The honest truth about Wix

These aren't things you can fix inside your Wix dashboard — they're built into the platform itself. The good news is that a custom-built site has none of this overhead. No unused JavaScript, no duplicate code, no platform tracking on your pages. A well-built custom site can load in under 1.5 seconds — more than four times faster than your site right now.
