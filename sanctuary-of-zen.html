Meet Olivia

About Sanctuary of Zen

Our Story

A space to properly switch off

I'm Olivia — a fully qualified Holistic Massage Therapist, Angelic Reiki Practitioner, and your local Temple Spa consultant for the Sheffield and Dronfield area.

I take care of your mind, body and soul with a range of treatments, using organic products wherever I can. Based at Amàre Hair & Beauty in Dronfield, I also host group wellness evenings for anyone wanting to share a little self-care with friends or family.

For me, this was never just about massage — it's about giving people permission to properly stop, even if it's only for an hour. Every treatment is unhurried, personalised to you, and treats you as a whole person rather than just a list of aches to fix.
