The big picture

Three perfect scores. One that isn't.

First, the good news: your SEO, Accessibility and Best Practices are all 100 out of 100. Your site is findable on Google, accessible to all users, and technically well-structured. For a business with your level of recognition — Michelin Guide 2026, Best Hotel Restaurant for 10 consecutive years — that matters. You're not invisible online.

The one area that needs attention is performance. Your site scores 71 on mobile — in the "needs work" zone — and there's one figure in particular that stands out.

Total page size · Google flags anything over 1.6 MB as "enormous"

Google flags any page over 1.6 MB as having an "enormous network payload". Your homepage comes in at 2,675 KB — 2.7 megabytes — which puts it well above that threshold. Every visitor on a mobile phone or slower connection has to download all of that before your page can fully load.

Google's recommended maximum for LCP is 2.5 seconds. At 4.8 seconds, someone searching for a boutique hotel in Sheffield, or looking to book a table at a Michelin-listed restaurant, is waiting nearly double that before they can see your site properly.

Context matters here

A 71 performance score might not sound alarming — but consider the context. Brocco is Michelin-listed, 10-time award-winning, and the only 5-star restaurant with rooms in Sheffield. The guests you're trying to attract are comparing you with other hotels and restaurants online, often on their phones. A 4.8-second load against a competitor who loads in 1.5 seconds is a meaningful difference in first impression.

What's causing it

Images, JavaScript, and the Wix platform

The audit found several specific causes — most of them platform-level issues from Wix, plus some image delivery problems that are adding significantly to the page size.

150 KiB unused JavaScript 149 KiB image savings available 27 KiB duplicate JavaScript 12 KiB legacy JavaScript 11 KiB unused CSS 2,675 KiB total page size 3 long main-thread tasks 405 internal timing measurements
  • ⚠️
    Images — 149 KiB saving available. Your images aren't being optimally compressed and sized for mobile screens. 149 kilobytes of savings are available just from better image delivery. This is likely a major contributor to both the 2.7 MB page size and the 4.8s LCP.
  • ⚠️
    150 KiB of unused JavaScript. Over 150 kilobytes of JavaScript code is being downloaded on every visit but never actually used. This is Wix platform code — tools and features loaded whether your pages need them or not.
  • ⚠️
    3 long main-thread tasks. The browser's main thread — the one that handles rendering your page — is being blocked by 3 long-running tasks. This is what causes the TBT of 70ms and contributes to that sluggish feel on lower-powered mobile devices.
  • ⚠️
    Duplicate and legacy JavaScript (27 + 12 KiB). Code that's loaded twice, and code written for older browsers that modern devices don't need — both adding to download size and load time on every visit.
  • ⚠️
    11 KiB unused CSS. Stylesheet code that's loaded but never applied to any element on the page — another Wix platform artefact.
  • ⚠️
    405 internal timing measurements. Wix monitors its own platform by running 405 performance measurements on every page visit. Invisible to you, but adds overhead to every single load.

What can be fixed inside Wix vs. what can't

The JavaScript issues — unused code, duplicates, legacy scripts, platform overhead — can't be fixed inside the Wix editor. They're baked into the platform. The image issues can sometimes be improved with compression and better upload practices, but the format and delivery optimisations that would make the biggest difference require moving to a custom-built site with full control over the asset pipeline.
