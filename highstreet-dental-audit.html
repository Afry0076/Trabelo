What's Going Wrong

Critical
Your site is slow to load on mobile — and patients notice
Performance scored 66/100. Google's own data says your Largest Contentful Paint takes 8.8 seconds on mobile.
Why it matters to you

Most people researching dental practices are doing it on their phone, in an evening. A performance score of 66 means your site takes the best part of 9 seconds to fully load on a typical mobile connection. Research consistently shows people abandon sites after 3 seconds. That's potential patients clicking onto a competitor before they've even seen what you offer.

What the fix looks like

A rebuilt site would properly compress and resize images before they're uploaded, remove the render-blocking CSS and JavaScript that's currently delaying the page, and be built clean from the ground up rather than running through WordPress with multiple plugins. The target for a rebuild would be 97+ on performance.

Critical
Images are far too large and slowing everything down
Google identified 1,040 KB of image savings possible — that's over 1MB of unnecessary data being loaded.
Why it matters to you

Your practice photos are beautiful — but they're being served at full resolution and without next-generation formatting, so a phone on 4G has to download a huge amount of data just to show the homepage. Every extra second of loading loses visitors.

What the fix looks like

On a rebuilt site, every image is compressed and converted to modern formats (WebP), sized correctly for the screen displaying them, and lazy-loaded so they only download when the patient scrolls to them. This alone typically takes 3–5 seconds off load time.

Critical
Files loading in the wrong order are delaying everything
Render-blocking resources are adding an estimated 2,950ms — nearly 3 seconds — to your first visible content.
Why it matters to you

Think of it like a checkout queue where every customer has to wait for something to arrive before the till can open. Your site is loading stylesheets and scripts in a way that stops anything from appearing on screen until they're all finished — so patients stare at a blank or half-loaded page for longer than they should.

What the fix looks like

A hand-coded rebuild doesn't have this problem — CSS and JavaScript are structured so the visual content loads first and any non-essential code loads in the background. It's part of how a 97+ performance score is achievable.

Moderate
286 KB of code that's never used is being loaded anyway
219 KB of unused CSS and 67 KB of unused JavaScript are being sent to every visitor, every time.
Why it matters to you

WordPress themes and page builders load their entire library of code even for pages that only use a fraction of it. It's the equivalent of downloading an entire encyclopedia when you only need one chapter — and your patients pay the price with slower load times.

What the fix looks like

A custom-built site only includes exactly the code it needs — nothing more. There's no bloat from unused theme features or third-party builders.

Moderate
Accessibility issues that could affect some of your patients
Score of 85/100 — several issues found including colour contrast, missing landmark structure, and heading order.
Why it matters to you

Accessibility means whether your site works well for everyone — including patients with visual impairments who use screen readers, or older patients who zoom in on their phones. Some of your text doesn't have enough colour contrast to be comfortably readable, and the page structure makes it harder for assistive technology to navigate. It also affects your SEO score.

What the fix looks like

A rebuild targets 98+ on accessibility — proper colour contrast, correct heading structure, alt text on all images, and a logical page layout that works with screen readers and keyboard navigation.

Minor
Six links on your site have no descriptive text
SEO score of 92 — Google found 6 links where the text doesn't tell it (or the user) where they lead.
Why it matters to you

Google crawls your site by following links and reading their text to understand what's on each page. Links with vague text like "Read more" or "Click here" don't tell Google anything useful — which means those pages get less credit. For a dental practice competing on terms like "dental implants Sheffield", every SEO point counts.

What the fix looks like

A rebuild ensures every link is descriptive — "Learn about dental implants in Sheffield" not just "Read more." This is standard on all Trabelo builds and contributes to an SEO score of 100.

Moderate
Patients on mobile can't zoom in on your site
Your site uses user-scalable="no" — which locks the zoom level and is flagged by Google as an accessibility failure.
Why it matters to you

Some of your patients — particularly older ones — rely on being able to pinch-zoom to read text on their phones. Locking that out is a barrier, and it's also against Google's accessibility guidelines. It's a small thing to fix, but it matters to real people.

What the fix looks like

Removing this single line and ensuring the site is properly mobile-responsive (so zooming is never needed) is straightforward on a rebuild.
