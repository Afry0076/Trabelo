What's Going Wrong

A plain-English breakdown of the main issues — what they are, why they matter, and what the fix looks like.

🐢
The site takes far too long to load on mobile
Performance: 51/100 · LCP: 16.6 seconds · FCP: 8.0 seconds
Critical

What's happening

Google measured your page taking 8 seconds before anything appears, and 16.6 seconds before the main content is visible. The performance score of 51 is firmly in the "poor" bracket. The biggest culprits are unoptimised images, unused JavaScript and CSS, and long main-thread tasks that block everything else from loading.

Why it matters to you

Most people looking for a cosmetic clinic are browsing on their phone in the evening. Research consistently shows that if a site takes longer than 3 seconds, most visitors leave. With 16+ seconds to fully load, you're losing a huge proportion of potential patients before they've even seen your treatments — including the Soprano Titanium, which is completely unique in South Yorkshire.

What the rebuild fixes

A purpose-built site would score 97+ on performance, loading in under 1.5 seconds on mobile. Images optimised, unnecessary code stripped out, nothing blocking the page from appearing instantly. Visitors see your clinic immediately — and stay long enough to book.

🖼️
Images are massively oversized and unoptimised
Estimated image savings: 773 KB · Total page size: 3,195 KB
Critical

What's happening

Google flagged 773KB of unnecessary image data — that's nearly three quarters of a megabyte of extra download on top of everything else. The total page is over 3MB, which is extremely heavy for a website homepage. Images aren't being properly compressed or served in efficient modern formats.

Why it matters to you

Treatment imagery and before/after photos are one of the most powerful tools a clinic like yours has. But if those images are making the site slow, they're actively working against you. Potential patients who can't see your results quickly will find a competitor whose site loads faster.

What the rebuild fixes

Every image would be properly compressed and delivered in modern WebP format — dramatically smaller files with no visible quality loss. Your treatment imagery and clinic photos would still look stunning, just loading in a fraction of the time.

⚙️
Too much unused JavaScript and CSS slowing everything down
Unused JS: 590 KB savings · Unused CSS: 132 KB savings · Main thread work: 2.3 seconds
Critical

What's happening

Your site is loading 590KB of JavaScript and 132KB of CSS that it doesn't actually use on the page. That's 722KB of dead code your visitors have to download every time they visit. On top of that, the browser's main processing thread is blocked for 2.3 seconds — meaning nothing else can happen while it works through it all.

Why it matters to you

This is typically caused by WordPress themes and plugins that add a lot of code "just in case" — code that's never actually used on your homepage. It bloats the page and significantly slows down the experience for every single visitor on every single visit.

What the rebuild fixes

A clean, custom-built site carries only the code it actually needs — nothing more. No bloated theme frameworks, no unused plugin scripts. The result is a fast, lean site that loads cleanly without all the background processing your current site demands.

♿
Accessibility issues — some patients struggle to use the site
Accessibility: 85/100 · ARIA errors, contrast issues, keyboard navigation problems
Important

What's happening

Google flagged several accessibility issues: ARIA elements (used by screen readers) with missing required children, input fields without accessible names, colour contrast that's too low in places, and elements with incorrectly configured keyboard navigation. There are also heading structure issues throughout.

Why it matters to you

Accessibility issues affect real patients — particularly older visitors or those with visual impairments who use screen readers or rely on keyboard navigation. A patient who can't easily use your site won't book. Google also uses accessibility as a ranking signal, so these issues can affect how visible your clinic is in search results.

What the rebuild fixes

The rebuilt site would score 98+ on accessibility. Proper ARIA labelling, sufficient colour contrast throughout, correct heading structure and keyboard-navigable elements. Every patient, regardless of ability, can use the site comfortably.

💾
Caching isn't working efficiently — repeat visitors reload everything
Cache savings: 91 KB · Returning visitors download the full site every time
Notable

What's happening

Browser caching tells a visitor's device to remember files from previous visits so it doesn't have to re-download them. Your site's caching isn't set up efficiently, meaning patients who have visited before still have to download most of the site from scratch every time they come back.

Why it matters to you

Returning patients — perhaps checking treatment prices, rebooking, or referring a friend — get a slow experience even though they've been to the site before. That's a poor second impression for someone who's already a fan of the clinic.

What the rebuild fixes

Proper caching configured from day one. A returning patient's browser already has most of the site saved locally — so the page loads almost instantly on repeat visits. A much better experience for your loyal patient base.
