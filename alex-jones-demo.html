Dental implants

3,000+ implants. 98% success rate.

Alex Jones Dentistry specialises in dental implantology — from a single tooth replacement to full arch restorations. Over 20 years of experience. ITI member.

🦷
Single dental implant
Replace a single missing tooth with a permanent, natural-looking implant that looks and feels exactly like your own tooth.
🦷🦷
Multiple implants
Restore several missing teeth with individual implants or implant-supported bridges — avoiding damage to healthy neighbouring teeth.
⚡
Teeth in a Day
Walk in with missing or failing teeth, walk out with a full set of fixed teeth — all on the same day as your implant surgery.
🦷
Zygomatic implants
An advanced solution for patients with severely reduced bone — zygomatic implants anchor to the cheekbone rather than the jaw.
💪
Bone grafting
If bone loss has occurred, bone grafting can rebuild the jaw to create a stable foundation for implants.
💰
Denture implants
Stabilise removable dentures with implants — no more slipping, clicking or adhesive. A life-changing solution for denture wearers.
