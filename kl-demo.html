What we offer

Our Treatments

Every treatment at KL is delivered by qualified aesthetic nurses using premium, clinically-proven products. We consult every client individually — no one-size-fits-all approach here.

Aesthetics

Non-Surgical Face Treatments

Anti-Ageing

Wrinkle-Relaxing (Botox®)

Softens expression lines, frown lines and crow's feet using a natural purified protein. Results are natural-looking and typically last 3–4 months.

Volume & Contour

Dermal Filler Treatments

Restores facial volume, reduces wrinkles and enhances contours. Only the finest filler brands, including Juvederm® and Teoxane.

Lip Enhancement

Lip Filler & Russian Lip

Hyaluronic acid fillers to add shape, definition and natural plumpness. Techniques include standard, Russian lip and Lip Flip.

Skin Booster

Profhilo®

A bioremodelling injectable that deeply hydrates the skin and stimulates collagen and elastin production — not a filler, a skin booster.

Advanced

Polynucleotides (PDRN)

Next-generation skin rejuvenation injections that repair and regenerate at a cellular level — excellent for skin quality, dark circles and fine lines.

Advanced

PRP & PRF Therapy

Platelet-rich plasma and fibrin therapy — using your body's own growth factors to rejuvenate skin and stimulate hair regrowth naturally.

Contouring

Nose, Temple & Tear Trough Filler

Specialist filler treatments for non-surgical nose reshaping, temple volume restoration and under-eye rejuvenation.

Signature

HArmonyCa® Hybrid Filler

The world's first hybrid filler combining immediate hyaluronic acid volume with gradual collagen biostimulation from calcium hydroxyapatite.

Regenerative

Purasome (Exosomes)

Cutting-edge exosome therapy that triggers the skin's natural repair processes — ideal for skin quality, hair restoration and post-treatment recovery.

Skin

Skin Treatments

Advanced Technology

Morpheus8 RF Microneedling

Radiofrequency microneedling that remodels skin and subdermal tissue. Tightens, lifts and rejuvenates — with minimal downtime.

Glow Facial

HydraFacial

Cleanse, extract and hydrate in one treatment for instantly glowing skin. No downtime, suitable for all skin types.

Premium Facial

The Million Dollar Facial

KL's luxury signature facial combining microneedling, LED therapy and a bespoke skincare regime for a truly transformative result.

Exfoliation

Dermaplaning

Manual exfoliation using a surgical blade to remove dead skin cells and vellus hair — leaving skin silky smooth and radiant.

Tightening

EndyMed Skin Tightening

Non-surgical radiofrequency skin tightening that lifts and firms loose skin on the face and body without any downtime.

Medical Grade

ZO® Stimulator Peel

A medical-grade chemical peel that improves skin texture, tone and clarity — designed by world-renowned dermatologist Dr Zein Obagi.

Laser & IPL

Laser & Light Treatments

Permanent Reduction

Laser Hair Removal

Clinical-grade laser technology for effective, long-lasting hair reduction on the face and body. Safe for a wide range of skin types and tones.

Skin Rejuvenation

Lumecca IPL

Intense pulsed light treatment targeting pigmentation, redness, sun damage and thread veins — one of the most powerful IPL devices available.

Plasma Resurfacing

NeoGen Plasma Treatment

Nitrogen plasma energy resurfacing that regenerates healthy skin — effective for wrinkles, laxity, acne scarring and general skin quality.

Kirsty Louise Whitworth

BMedSci (Hons) RGN · Aesthetic Nurse Practitioner · Independent Prescriber

KL Cosmetic Clinics was set up by Kirsty Louise Whitworth — an advanced aesthetic nurse practitioner and independent nurse prescriber, registered with the Nursing and Midwifery Council.

For over 10 years, Kirsty held nursing and management positions within the NHS. Since leaving, she has trained to advanced level with some of the UK's leading surgeons and cosmetic nurses.

She continues to update her skills by attending regular advanced training, manufacturer workshops and industry conferences — ensuring her clients always benefit from the very latest procedures and products.
