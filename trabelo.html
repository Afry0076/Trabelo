Before you ask

Questions people usually have

If yours isn't here, that's what WhatsApp is for.

Is the audit actually free?

Yes — no card details, no strings, no follow-up sales calls if you'd rather not hear from me again. You get the real numbers either way.

Why do you build the demo before I pay anything?

Because it's the only honest way to prove the work is good. You get to click through a real working site, not a mockup or a sales deck, before you decide anything.

Why is this so much cheaper than other web agencies?

No office, no account managers, no work farmed out to someone else. It's just me, working directly with you — I'd rather build up a lot of good relationships across Sheffield than charge agency prices for the same result.

What if I don't like the demo?

Then it costs you nothing. No charge, no pressure, no obligation to explain why.

Do I need to know anything technical?

No. Everything's explained in plain English, and once your site's live you won't need to touch any code to keep it running.

What happens after the site goes live?

On the Starter tier, small updates are £15 each, or £30/month for unlimited small changes. Business and Premium are built with more flexibility from the start.

What's the 14-day guarantee actually cover?

If something's broken, or the site doesn't match what we agreed before you paid, I'll fix it free within 14 days of going live — no charge, no argument. It's not an open-ended "change my mind" clause — that's what the free demo stage is for — it's a promise that what gets delivered is what we agreed.

Do you only work with South Yorkshire businesses?

No — I'm based in Sheffield and know South Yorkshire best, but I build websites for small businesses anywhere in the UK. The whole process works remotely: audit, demo and updates all happen over email/WhatsApp.
