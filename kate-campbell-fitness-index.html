What We Offer

Everything in one place.

At Kate Campbell Fitness, you don't need to go elsewhere. From your first workout to long-term health, we have the expertise to support you at every stage.

🏋️
Training

Personal Training

1-1, 2-1 or group PT — all affordable, flexible and tailored entirely to you. Choose block bookings, monthly payments or pay upfront. We listen to your goals first, then build a programme that actually achieves them.

💆
Recovery

Move Well — Therapy & Massage

Sports therapy, manual therapy and massage — from getting strong for surgery (prehabilitation) to recovering from injury or a procedure. Our therapists take a whole-person approach, combining exercise programmes with hands-on treatment.

🍎
Nutrition

Nutritional Guidance

Nutrition is a minefield to navigate alone. We help you cut through the conflicting information and find a sustainable approach that works for your life — not just a short-term fix.

Women's Health

From pre-pregnancy
to the menopause years.

Kate spent over 20 years as a Sheffield community and hospital midwife before founding KCF. That knowledge underpins everything we do for women's health and wellbeing.

🤰
Pregnancy Health & Wellbeing
Safe, supported exercise throughout pregnancy — guided by Kate's 20+ years of midwifery experience. Prenatal classes and one-to-one sessions available.
🌱
Postnatal Recovery
A gradual, safe return to exercise after birth. Postnatal classes, wellbeing assessments and one-to-one support — when your body is ready, at a pace that works for you.
🌸
The Meno Years
Strength and wellness programmes built around the needs of women going through perimenopause and menopause — energy, bone density, mood and confidence.
👧
Teen Personal Training
Safe, age-appropriate fitness for teenagers — building confidence, strength and healthy habits at the right time in their development.
🏠
Fit@Home

About Kate

Midwife to
Gym Owner.

Before founding Kate Campbell Fitness, Kate spent over 20 years working as a community and hospital midwife in Sheffield. That experience — caring for women, listening to their needs, understanding their bodies — shaped everything KCF has become.

The result is a gym that doesn't just care about your fitness. It cares about you as a person, at every stage of your life. From the moment you enquire, Kate's team works to understand what you actually need — not what a generic programme assumes you need.

The wider team includes Personal Trainers, Sports Therapists, class instructors and online coaches — all working together with the same philosophy Kate started with: listen first, coach properly, support always.
