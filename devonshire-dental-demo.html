About the Practice

A Practice Built
on Genuine Care

Devonshire Quarter Dental is a well-established family-run NHS and private practice right in the heart of Sheffield. We believe going to the dentist should never feel daunting — and our team goes out of their way to make sure it doesn't.

Whether you've been coming to us for years or you're walking through the door for the first time, you'll always be made to feel welcome.

Kind and unhurried

We take the time to explain your options and make sure you're comfortable before we begin.

Great for anxious patients

Catriona specialises in patients who find dental visits difficult, including children and those with dental phobia.

Central Sheffield location

Easy to reach from anywhere in the city — we're right in the Devonshire Quarter.
