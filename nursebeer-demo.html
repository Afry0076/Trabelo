Our story
Expert, honest, affordable aesthetics

Cosmetic Clinic is a private aesthetic clinic located off the Sheffield Parkway in Woodhouse, Sheffield S13 — run and led by Independent Nurse Practitioner Louise Beer RN INP.

Louise has 16 years of experience with aesthetic injectable treatments and has built the clinic through word-of-mouth recommendation. The loyal patient base that has grown over those years is testament to an approach defined by honesty, care and natural results.

The clinic operates by appointment only — offering a professional, private and personal service where every treatment plan is designed specifically for you.
