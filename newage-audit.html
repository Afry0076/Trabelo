What's Going Wrong

Plain-English breakdown of the main issues — what they are, why they matter to your clinic, and what the fix looks like.

🐢
The site loads far too slowly on mobile
Performance: 58/100 · LCP: 6.0 seconds · TBT: 530ms · Total page: 3,529 KB
Critical

What's happening

Google measured 6 seconds before your main content appears on a phone — and the Total Blocking Time of 530ms means the page is frozen and unresponsive for over half a second during loading. The total page size is 3.5MB. There are 8 long main-thread tasks, 754KB of unused JavaScript, 184KB of unused CSS, and 619KB of data not being cached properly. Most of this is Wix platform overhead.

Why it matters to you

Most people searching for laser hair removal, Plasma Pen treatments or fillers in Doncaster are browsing on their phones in the evening. At 6 seconds to load, the vast majority will have given up and moved on long before they've seen your treatment list or your reviews. With 30+ treatments available, every page load that fails is a booking lost that you'll never know about.

What the rebuild fixes

A custom-built site free of Wix overhead would score 97+ on performance — loading in under 1.5 seconds on mobile. No unused code, no unnecessary scripts, images properly optimised. Visitors see the site instantly and stay long enough to discover everything you offer.

⚠️
Best Practices score of 73 — third-party cookies and browser errors
Third-party cookies found · Browser errors logged · Security headers missing
Critical

What's happening

Google flagged third-party cookies on your site, browser console errors on every page load, and issues logged in Chrome's developer panel. There are also missing security headers (CSP, HSTS, COOP and others) that Google flags as best practice deficiencies. A score of 73 is the lowest I've seen across the South Yorkshire clinics I've audited.

Why it matters to you

Third-party cookies are increasingly blocked by browsers, which can break functionality. Console errors suggest parts of the site aren't working correctly behind the scenes. Missing security headers make the site more vulnerable to certain types of attack and are flagged by Google as a trust concern. All of this combined drags the best practices score to a level that reflects poorly on an otherwise professional clinic.

What the rebuild fixes

A clean custom build has no third-party cookie dependencies, zero console errors, and proper security headers configured from day one. Best Practices would jump to 96+ — in line with what the clinic's quality deserves.

🏗️
Wix is putting a ceiling on what's possible
619KB uncached · 36KB duplicate JS · 77KB legacy JS · Wix platform overhead
Important

What's happening

The site is built on Wix, which loads its own scripts, legacy JavaScript (code written for older browsers that modern ones don't need), duplicate JavaScript files and a large amount of data with no caching set up. These are structural issues that come with the platform — they can't be fully resolved within Wix regardless of how the content is arranged.

Why it matters to you

A performance score of 58 is likely close to the ceiling for what Wix can achieve on your site. Even with image optimisation and content changes, the platform overhead will keep the score suppressed. It also means you're paying Wix a monthly subscription for a platform that's actively limiting how well your business performs online.

What the rebuild fixes

A custom-coded site has no platform overhead. Every line of code is there because it's needed — no legacy scripts, no duplicated files, no platform bloat. The performance, best practices and overall speed all jump dramatically as a result. And there's no monthly Wix subscription to replace.

♿
Small accessibility issues worth tidying up
Accessibility: 92/100 · iFrame missing title · Touch targets · Heading structure
Minor

What's happening

Your accessibility score of 92 is good — better than most. Google flagged a few minor remaining issues: an embedded frame (likely the booking widget) without a title for screen readers, some touch targets that are slightly too small on mobile, and heading elements that aren't in the correct sequential order.

Why it matters to you

92 is solid, but these are easy wins that would push the score to 98+. Correctly labelled frames help screen readers navigate your booking system, properly sized touch targets make booking easier on phones, and correct heading structure helps both accessibility tools and Google understand your page content.

What the rebuild fixes

All three issues resolved properly from day one — frame titles, touch target sizing and correct heading hierarchy. Accessibility goes from 92 to 98+, ticking off the last remaining issue across all four Google metrics.
