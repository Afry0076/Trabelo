Our services

Personal legal support for life's most important moments.

We specialise in the legal matters that matter most — and we handle every one with the personal attention and care that each client deserves.

📜

Wills

Your will is the document that ensures your wishes are carried out. Without one, the law decides what happens to your estate — which may not be what you'd choose. We make the process straightforward and ensure everything is properly documented.

  • Single & mirror wills
  • Complex estate wills
  • Reviewing & updating existing wills
  • Storage & safekeeping
📋

Lasting Powers of Attorney

A Lasting Power of Attorney is a legal document that lets you appoint someone you trust to make decisions on your behalf if you ever lose capacity. There are two types — property & financial affairs, and health & welfare — and we recommend having both.

  • Property & financial affairs LPA
  • Health & welfare LPA
  • Registration with the Office of the Public Guardian
  • Advice on choosing your attorneys
🏛️

Probate & Estate Administration

When someone passes away, their estate needs to be administered and distributed. We handle the entire probate process — from applying for the grant of probate to distributing the estate — compassionately and efficiently.

  • Applying for grant of probate
  • Full estate administration
  • Inheritance tax advice & returns
  • Intestacy (no will) cases
🛡️

Trusts & Tax Planning

Trusts can protect your assets, reduce inheritance tax liability and ensure your estate passes to the right people in the right way. We advise on the full range of trust arrangements and long-term estate planning strategies.

  • Discretionary & bare trusts
  • Inheritance tax planning
  • Asset protection strategies
  • Trust administration
🏠

Residential Conveyancing

We're CQS-accredited — the Law Society's quality standard for conveyancing. Whether you're buying, selling, remortgaging or transferring ownership, we handle the legal work carefully and keep you informed throughout.

  • Buying & selling residential property
  • Remortgaging
  • Transfer of equity
  • Freehold & leasehold
👩‍⚕️

Managing Your Affairs

For older clients or those who need support, we offer guidance on managing legal affairs — from Court of Protection applications to advice on care home funding and deputyship.

  • Court of Protection
  • Deputyship applications
  • Care funding advice
  • Home visits available
About us

A Sheffield firm built on trust — since 1993.

Rosalind Watchorn set up the firm in 1993 with a clear aim: to give people a more personal approach to the legal matters they face at important points in life. More than 30 years later, that's still what we do.

We've grown almost entirely through word of mouth and personal recommendations — which tells you something about how we work. We don't pay referral fees. We earn our clients through the quality of our service and the care we show them.

Every client is assigned their own specialist who stays with them throughout the process. We offer home visits for clients who can't come to us, and a free 30-minute initial consultation for anyone who wants to understand their options before committing to anything.
