Our story

A Broomhill landmark, warmed up.

For decades, 184 Whitham Road was the local bank. Now the old banking hall smells of fresh coffee and warm simit, and the only thing we count is how many pancakes leave the kitchen.

  1. The bank yearsA building dating back to around the 1850s. It was a Williams & Glyn's branch, then RBS, until it closed in November 2018.
  2. 2022: The Grind arrivesThe boards came down, and "The Grind Café Broomhill" on the windows was the first name on the building since the bank left.
  3. Today: family-runA Turkish-influenced kitchen, pastries baked in-house every day, and a full menu from 8am.
