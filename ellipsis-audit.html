What's Going Wrong

Plain English breakdown — what each issue is, why it matters, and what a rebuild fixes.

♿
Accessibility issues — 77/100 is the biggest concern
Missing image alt text · Contrast issues · Broken ARIA · Touch targets too small
Most Important

What's happening

Google flagged several accessibility issues: images have no descriptions for screen readers, some text doesn't have enough contrast to be legible in certain conditions, ARIA attributes (used by assistive technology) are incorrectly configured, links don't have clear names, and touch targets on mobile are too small to tap comfortably. There are also heading structure issues.

Why it matters to you

Accessibility issues affect real patients — particularly older visitors or those using screen readers or keyboard navigation. If part of your site is hard to use, those patients won't book. It also affects your Google ranking — accessibility is a signal Google uses when deciding where to place sites in search results. For two clinics competing for the same local searches, every ranking point matters.

What the rebuild fixes

A rebuilt site would score 98+ on accessibility. Every image properly described, sufficient contrast throughout, correct ARIA labelling, properly sized touch targets and a clean heading structure. Every patient — whatever device or ability — can use the site without friction.

⏱️
Performance at 88 — good, but not where it needs to be
LCP: 3.3s · FCP: 2.7s · Unused JS: 96KB · Unused CSS: 92KB
Important

What's happening

The main content takes 3.3 seconds to appear on mobile — Google's threshold for "good" is under 2.5 seconds. The site is also carrying 96KB of unused JavaScript and 92KB of unused CSS, plus 4 long main-thread tasks and 10 non-composited animations that slow things down further. The page is doing more work than it needs to on every visit.

Why it matters to you

88 is closer to good than most, but patients browsing on their phones at home will still notice that extra second of loading. More importantly, Google rewards faster sites with better search rankings — and for two clinics trying to show up for "aesthetics Doncaster" and "aesthetics near me" searches, a performance score in the 97+ range gives you an edge over competitors still sitting at 88 or below.

What the rebuild fixes

A clean, custom-built site with no bloated unused code would hit 97+ on performance. Images optimised, animations composited properly, no unnecessary scripts. The page loads in under a second on most connections — and Google notices the difference.

🔍
SEO at 92 — a couple of fixable issues limiting Google visibility
Missing image alt text · Could affect search rankings
Notable

What's happening

Several images across the site have no alt text — the descriptive label that tells Google what an image is about. Google can't "see" images, so without this text it can't index them properly. This limits how well those pages rank in image search and contributes to the 92 rather than 100 score.

Why it matters to you

For a business with two clinic locations, SEO is your main free source of new patients. Every fix that brings the score closer to 100 means your clinic appears higher when someone in Doncaster searches for treatments you offer. 92 is decent but there are specific, easy wins being left on the table.

What the rebuild fixes

A rebuilt site would score 100 on SEO. Every image properly labelled, correct page structure, fast loading (which Google rewards heavily), clean heading hierarchy. Done right from day one — not as an afterthought.

💾
Caching and layout shifts adding unnecessary friction
Cache savings: 84KB · CLS: 0.036 · Layout shift culprits flagged
Minor

What's happening

Caching isn't configured optimally — returning visitors re-download 84KB of content they've already seen. There's also a Cumulative Layout Shift score of 0.036, which means elements on the page are moving slightly after the initial load — a jarring experience Google specifically flags as a negative user experience.

Why it matters to you

Returning patients — comparing treatments, checking prices, booking a second appointment — have to wait for the site to reload from scratch. Layout shifts feel unprofessional, especially on a mobile phone where a button or image suddenly jumping position can cause accidental taps and frustration.

What the rebuild fixes

Proper caching from day one and zero layout shifts — every element sized and positioned correctly before the page loads. A smoother, more polished experience for new and returning patients alike.
