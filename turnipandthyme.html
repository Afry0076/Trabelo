Welcome

A Labour of Love
from Robert & Louise

Nestled on Ecclesall Road near Hunter's Bar, Turnip & Thyme is a cosy, family-run bistro offering British comfort food traditions with a seasonal twist and a touch of international flair — all served in a laid-back, inviting atmosphere.

Our menu echoes the rhythm of the seasons, lovingly prepared with ingredients curated from local farmers and suppliers who share our dedication to responsibility and excellence.

Our Story

A Labour of Love on Ecclesall Road

Robert & Louise

Nestled on Ecclesall Road near the Hunter's Bar roundabout, our bistro is a labour of love from the sibling duo, Robert and Louise. Our heart lies in creating a relaxed, inviting atmosphere where the tradition of British food thrives.

Our menu, echoing the rhythm of the seasons, is a tribute to our commitment to quality. Our dishes are lovingly prepared with ingredients curated from local farmers and suppliers who share our dedication to responsibility and excellence.

From succulent pork belly to traditional Sunday roasts, from our famous seafood nights to seasonal specials — there's something to entice every palate, whether it be for a simple meal or a special occasion.
