Which is right for Jam?

Fresha makes more sense if…

  • Lots of your new clients find you in the Fresha app.That's the one thing a website can't copy. If it's most of them, weigh that up first.
  • You want deposits or no-show fees taken by card.The website doesn't take payments.
  • You'd rather not change anything.Switching takes a few weeks of care, and we won't pretend it doesn't.

Your own site makes more sense if…

  • Most clients are regulars or come by word of mouth.With 272 five-star reviews, that's likely. Then you're paying Fresha mainly for a diary.
  • You want the monthly bill gone.One payment, then roughly £12 a year.
  • You want clients to book with Jam, not a marketplace.Your name, your rules, no other salons next to you.
Not sure how many new clients Fresha sends you? Fresha's reports show where clients came from. That number is the best guide to this whole decision.
