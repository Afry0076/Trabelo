Buying a home?

Only four things really matter.

A report can feel overwhelming. Most of it is maintenance tips and minor wear. The issues worth acting on fall into four categories.

1Major defectsFor example, a structural failure.
2Things that lead to major defectsLike a small roof-flashing leak.
3Finance, occupancy & insuranceAnything that could stop you financing, legally occupying or insuring the home.
4Safety hazardsSuch as an exposed, live bus bar in the electrical panel.

For estate agents & sellers

Inspectors don't kill deals. Surprises do.

Since mandatory disclosure forms came in during 2022, a pre-sale inspection helps sellers complete them honestly and fully. Buyers feel protected, sellers face less risk of non-disclosure claims, and agents are seen as transparent and trustworthy.

  • Price the property with confidence
  • Complete disclosure forms properly
  • Fewer last-minute surprises at offer stage
  • An independent, unbiased report
