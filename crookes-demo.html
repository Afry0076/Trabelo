Friday nights

Sheffield Jazz at Crookes.

One of South Yorkshire's longest-running and best-loved jazz programmes, hosted here on Friday nights. Open to everyone — you don't need to be a member of anything.

Live jazz, every Friday.

Sheffield Jazz brings touring and local musicians to the club through the season. It's a proper listening room night — a full bar, a decent stage and an audience that actually listens.

Friday is one of the busiest nights of the week here. Alongside the jazz there's quiz and bingo running in the lounge, and the bar is open until midnight.

  • Friday nights through the Sheffield Jazz season
  • Bar open until midnight on Fridays
  • Open to all — no club membership required
  • Full programme and tickets via Sheffield Jazz

The Comedy Village.

Monthly comedy nights featuring professional stand-up acts from across the country — in the concert room, at club prices.

A proper night out.

The Comedy Village runs monthly comedy nights at Crookes Club, bringing touring stand-ups to a 500-capacity room in S10 — for a fraction of what the same bill costs in a city-centre venue.

Bar open through the show, table seating in the concert room and a properly lit stage. Tickets go through The Comedy Village directly.

  • Monthly, in the 500-capacity concert room
  • Professional touring stand-up acts
  • Club prices — a full night out for less than town
  • Bar open throughout, table seating available
  • Open to all — no membership needed

About

The nightspot of the north.

Come and join us.

Once described as the nightspot of the north, we offer something for everyone here at Crookes Club. From bowls to dancing, exercise classes to live music, we aim to make your week that little bit more exciting.

With a concert room able to hold 500 people, as well as a spacious lounge and bar area, Crookes Club is the place to be for any number of different events. Or, if you'd rather have a more leisurely evening, we also have two full-sized snooker tables and one pool table inside the club.

With regular live singers and bands we offer a fantastic place to spend your evening. Renowned as the number one social club in Sheffield, with fantastic TripAdvisor reviews, Crookes Club is currently the number one live music venue in Sheffield. We're also very proud to have won 2019 Entertainment Club of the Year.

If you just fancy getting out of the house and making some new friends, we take part in pool and snooker leagues on Monday and Wednesday evenings respectively.

Open to all

You do not need to be a member to use the club.

We always welcome new faces, so if you want to find out what club life is like, feel free to visit during normal opening hours. There's no membership, no joining fee and no sign-up — just walk in.
