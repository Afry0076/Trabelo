Our story
The nightspot of the north.

Crookes Club is Sheffield's premier social club — located on Mulehouse Road in Crookes, S10, and open to absolutely everyone. You don't need to be a member, you don't need to sign up. Just come along.

Once described as the nightspot of the north, we are Sheffield's No.1 rated live music venue on TripAdvisor and winner of the 2019 Entertainment Club of the Year. Our 500-capacity concert room has hosted everyone from local acts to sold-out tribute nights — and our lounge has live performers every Friday and Saturday.

But Crookes Club is more than live music. It's Zumba on Tuesday morning and jazz on Friday night. It's baby sensory and Gamers Night and comedy evenings and quiz nights. It's snooker leagues and bowls and dance classes. It's a genuine community hub in the heart of S10.
