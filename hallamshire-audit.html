What's Not Working — and Why It Matters

Five specific issues found on your site, in plain terms.

Critical

Performance score of 46 — main content takes 15 seconds to appear on mobile

What's happening

Google's mobile test shows the Largest Contentful Paint is 15.4 seconds — the main content takes over 15 seconds to appear on a phone. The first thing to appear takes 7.1 seconds. There are 719KB of unused JavaScript, 153KB of unused CSS, a further 549KB of image savings available, and 25 non-composited animations adding to the load. The site is carrying over 4MB of data per visit — around 4x more than it needs to.

What this means for your patients

Many of the conditions Hallamshire treats — neurological conditions, chronic pain, vestibular disorders — are suffered by people who may already be anxious or frustrated when they search for help. A 15-second wait before your site shows anything is a significant barrier. With over half of mobile users leaving after 3 seconds, the specialist treatments and awards the site promotes may never get seen.

The fix

A rebuilt site with no bloated plugins, properly compressed images, no unused code and no render-blocking files. Target: 97+ performance. Under 2 seconds on mobile.

Critical

Best Practices score of 54 — 5 insecure HTTP requests and 5 third-party cookies flagged

What's happening

Google found 5 insecure HTTP requests on the site — files loading without encryption — and 5 third-party cookies being set, plus browser errors and issues logged in Chrome DevTools. Several security headers are also missing (CSP, HSTS, COOP, Trusted Types). Together these bring the Best Practices score to 54 out of 100.

What this means for your patients

For a healthcare provider handling sensitive patient enquiries, insecure requests are particularly worth addressing. Third-party cookies can also trigger cookie consent issues. And the missing security headers are standard expectations on any modern professional website — especially one in healthcare.

The fix

Full HTTPS throughout, all security headers configured, no unnecessary third-party cookies. Target: 90+.

Accessibility

Accessibility score of 83 — contrast issues, missing labels and non-focusable skip links

What's happening

Google found colour contrast issues across the site (some text is insufficiently distinct from its background), links that rely on colour alone to be distinguishable, iframes without titles, links without discernible names, and skip links that aren't keyboard-focusable. Accessibility sits at 83 out of 100.

What this means for your patients

Hallamshire treats patients with neurological conditions, balance disorders, ME, and older adults — many of whom may use screen readers, keyboard navigation or magnification tools. Accessibility issues directly affect the people most likely to need your most specialist services. That's a meaningful gap to close.

The fix

Proper contrast ratios, labelled elements, keyboard-accessible navigation. Target: 98+.

SEO

SEO score of 85 — uncrawlable links and non-descriptive link text affecting Google's view of your site

What's happening

Google flagged 3 links with no descriptive text and that some links are not crawlable — meaning Google's search crawlers can't follow them to understand what pages they point to. This affects how well your specialist treatment pages are indexed and ranked.

What this means for your patients

If a potential patient searches "vestibular physiotherapy Sheffield" or "PD Warrior Sheffield" — and Google can't crawl those pages properly — you may not appear. With the breadth of specialist services Hallamshire offers, every uncrawlable link is a missed opportunity to rank for specialist terms your competitors don't cover.

The fix

Standard HTML links throughout, all descriptive and fully crawlable. SEO target: 100.

Performance

25 non-composited animations and 8 long blocking tasks adding to the sluggishness

What's happening

Google flagged 25 non-composited animated elements — animations running on the main thread rather than the GPU, causing stutter and blocking the page. There are also 8 long main-thread tasks and JavaScript execution taking over 1.3 seconds. These are all contributing to the overall sluggish feel on mobile.

What this means for your patients

Even after the site finishes loading, these animations make it feel janky and unresponsive — particularly on lower-powered mobile devices.

The fix

Deliberate, GPU-composited animations only where they genuinely add value. No animation for its own sake. Clean, fast and smooth on every device.
