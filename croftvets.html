What We Offer

Complete Veterinary Care for Your Pet

From routine check-ups to complex surgery — Croft Vets provides a comprehensive range of veterinary services at both our Bolsover and Sheffield centres.

🩺

General Services

Consultations, vaccinations, microchipping, parasite control, health checks and routine treatments — all delivered with care and attention at both our centres.

✂️

Surgical Services

From routine neutering and dental work to specialist orthopaedic surgery — we provide a broad spectrum of surgical procedures with full anaesthetic monitoring.

🔬

Diagnostics

State-of-the-art ultrasonography, endoscopy and radiographic imaging alongside modern in-house blood analysers. Answers faster, without unnecessary referrals.

💊

Wellness & Prevention

Vet and nurse consultations, weight clinics, dental checks and blood sampling to keep your pet healthy for longer. Preventative care that pays off.

🌍

Animal Health Certificates

Taking your pet abroad? We issue official Animal Health Certificates (AHCs) for travel to EU countries — book in advance and we'll guide you through the requirements.

🚨

Emergency Care

Emergencies don't keep office hours. Both our centres have emergency capability during opening hours, with out-of-hours provision through our trusted partners.

Our Services

Everything Your Pet Needs, All in One Place

Croft Vets provides a full range of veterinary services at both our Bolsover and Sheffield (Totley) centres. Whatever your pet needs — we're here for it.

🩺

General Consultations

Health checks, vaccinations, parasite control, microchipping and routine treatments. Open six days a week at both centres.

✂️

Surgical Services

Routine operations (neutering, dental, mass removal) through to specialist orthopaedic procedures — all with full anaesthetic monitoring and post-op care.

🔬

Advanced Diagnostics

Ultrasonography, endoscopy, radiographic (X-ray) imaging and modern in-house blood analysers. Fast results, fewer referrals.

🌍

Animal Health Certificates

Official AHCs for pet travel to EU countries. We'll walk you through all the requirements and make sure your pet is cleared to travel.

💉

Nurse Clinics

Nurse-led clinics for nail clips, weight checks, flea & worm treatments, post-op wound checks and much more — often without needing a full vet appointment.

🚨

Emergency & Out of Hours

Emergency care available during opening hours at both centres. Out-of-hours provision handled by our trusted external partner — call your local centre for details.

🦷

Dental Care

Dental health checks, professional cleaning, extractions and advice on at-home dental care. Dental disease is one of the most common preventable conditions in pets.

🐾

Puppy & Kitten Packages

First vaccinations, microchipping, parasite control and a full new-pet health check. The best start for your new arrival.

📋

Symptom & Poisons Guides

Not sure if your pet needs to be seen? Our online symptom guide and poisons/hazards guide help you make the right call quickly.

Our Story

Professional · Personal · Independent

Since 1992, Croft Vets has been serving pets and their owners across Sheffield and the surrounding areas. We're an independent, family-run practice — and we intend to stay that way. You'll never be just a number here.

Our handpicked team are highly trained, skilful and passionate individuals who devote themselves to patient care. Whether it's a routine appointment or a life-saving emergency, we bring the same level of attention and expertise every time.
