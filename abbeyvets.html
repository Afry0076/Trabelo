Our Story

Caring for Yorkshire's Pets Since 1975

Abbey Vet Group was founded in Barnsley in 1975. Over five decades, we've grown from a single practice to a network of eight branches across Barnsley and Sheffield — all while staying firmly rooted in the local communities we serve.

We're proud to be an RCVS-approved practice. Every member of our team — from our vets to our nurses and receptionists — is committed to providing the kind of care you'd want for your own pet.

In that time we've treated dogs, cats, rabbits, guinea pigs, birds and a great many other animals. We've helped families through difficult decisions and celebrated countless recoveries. That relationship with the South Yorkshire community is something we take seriously.
