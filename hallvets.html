What We Do

A Full Range of Care for Every Pet

From routine check-ups to specialist treatments — Hall Court Vets provides everything your pet needs under one roof, across three convenient locations.

🩺

General Consultations

Full health checks, vaccinations, microchipping and routine treatments. Open surgery and appointment sessions every weekday and Saturday morning.

💉

Nurse Clinics

Daily nurse-led clinics by appointment. Nail clips, puppy checks, flea & worming treatments, anal gland care and some repeat injections — all with our qualified nurses.

❤️

Senior Pet Health Checks

Comprehensive health checks for older cats and dogs — full blood screen, white and red cell count, liver and kidney function, urine analysis, and a full RVN consultation.

🔬

In-House Diagnostics

On-site laboratory and X-ray facilities so we can get answers quickly without sending your pet — or you — to a referral centre unnecessarily.

🌙

Out of Hours & Emergency

In-house emergency cover until 9pm Monday to Friday and 8am–12pm Saturday and Sunday. Outside those hours, our trusted external provider takes over. Always call 01909 562319.

🛁

Stable Spa

Our unique Stable Spa grooming service — bathing, styling and pampering for your pet in a calm, caring environment right on site at our Dinnington surgery.

Our Services

Everything Your Pet Needs, All in One Place

Hall Court Vets is a full-service small animal practice. From puppies and kittens to senior pets and emergencies — our experienced team is here for every stage of your pet's life.

🩺

General Consultations

Open surgery and appointments for health checks, vaccinations, microchipping, parasite control and more. Open six days a week across our surgeries.

💉

Nurse Clinics

Book a dedicated nurse clinic slot for nail clips, puppy checks, flea & worming, anal gland care and certain repeat injections — all delivered by our qualified RVNs.

❤️

Senior Health Checks

For older cats and dogs: a full blood screen checking white and red cells, liver and kidney function, plus urine analysis and an RVN-led consultation.

🔬

In-House Diagnostics

Laboratory blood testing and X-ray on site at Dinnington. Faster results, less waiting, fewer unnecessary referrals.

✂️

Surgery & Anaesthesia

Soft tissue and orthopaedic procedures carried out on site with full anaesthetic monitoring. Pre-op and post-op care included as standard.

🐶

Puppy & Kitten Club

Start your new pet's life right. Puppy and kitten health checks, first vaccinations, microchipping and preventative advice from our nursing team.

🌙

Out of Hours Emergency

In-house emergency cover until 9pm Monday–Friday and 8am–12pm Saturday & Sunday. Overnight and outside those hours: our external provider is always on hand. Call 01909 562319 any time.

🛁

Stable Spa

Professional pet grooming right at our Dinnington surgery. Bathing, styling and coat care in a calm, stress-free environment. Book by phone.

🛒

Pet Shop

Quality pet food, accessories and veterinary-approved products available in practice. Prescription diets also stocked at Dinnington.

Who We Are

A Century of Care in South Yorkshire

Hall Court Veterinary Group has been part of the fabric of South Yorkshire for over 100 years. Based in the grounds of the Old Hall stable block in Dinnington since the 1960s, we've built our reputation one patient at a time — providing personal, professional veterinary care that the big corporate chains simply can't match.

We're an independently owned practice, which means you deal with the same team every time. We know your pet's history. We know your family. That's the difference between a practice that cares and one that's just going through the motions.

Facilities

Our Dinnington Surgery

Set in the grounds of the historic Old Hall stable block at 1 St. Leonard's Close, our main Dinnington surgery is a unique and memorable setting. Facilities include consulting rooms, a surgical suite, in-house laboratory, X-ray, a ward for inpatients, and the Stable Spa grooming salon.

We also have a range of downloadable client information handouts — from pet care guides to vaccination schedules. Ask at reception or visit our website.
