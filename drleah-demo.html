What we offer

Our Treatments

Natural, holistic, undetectable. Every treatment at Dr Leah's is chosen for its quality and safety, and every patient receives a bespoke treatment plan — never one-size-fits-all.

Anti-Wrinkle

Botox

Botulinum toxin anti-wrinkle injections to soften expression lines, frown lines and crow's feet. Always natural-looking — Dr Leah's ethos is undetectable results.

Volume & Contour

Dermal Fillers

Hyaluronic acid filler for facial volume, lip enhancement and contouring. Tailored to your individual facial structure and personal aesthetic goals.

Skin Booster

Profhilo

A bioremodelling injectable — not a standard filler — that deeply hydrates and stimulates collagen and elastin production for naturally tighter, more radiant skin.

Regenerative

Polynucleotides (PDRN)

Cutting-edge injections that repair and regenerate skin at a cellular level. Outstanding for skin quality, dark circles, under-eye concerns and fine lines.

Biostimulator

Sculptra

Poly-L-lactic acid collagen stimulator for gradual, long-lasting volume and skin quality improvement. Results build naturally over weeks for an authentically refreshed look.

Microneedling

Skin Pen Microneedling

Advanced precision microneedling to trigger the skin's natural healing and boost collagen — improves texture, tone, scarring and overall radiance.

Cryotherapy

CryoPen

Precise cryotherapy for the treatment of skin blemishes including skin tags, warts, verrucae, milia and age spots — quick, effective and minimal downtime.

Medical Peels

AlumierMD Chemical Peels

Medical-grade chemical peels from the AlumierMD range — targeting pigmentation, acne, skin texture and dullness for a clearer, more even complexion.

Medical Skincare

AlumierMD Medical Grade Skincare

Bespoke medical-grade skincare programmes from AlumierMD — complementing your in-clinic treatments and maintaining results at home.

Health

Mole Screening

Professional mole assessment and screening using Dr Leah's medical expertise — providing peace of mind and early detection of any concerns requiring further care.
