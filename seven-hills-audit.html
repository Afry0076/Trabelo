Hi — I'm Aidan. I'm 18 and I do web design across Sheffield and South Yorkshire. I came across Seven Hills Physiotherapy while looking at physio clinic websites in the area and ran your site through Google's speed testing tool.

The good news is your performance score of 84 is the best of the clinics I've audited in the area — the site loads reasonably well. But there are some specific issues around accessibility, security and how Google crawls your site that are worth addressing. Here's what I found, and what a rebuilt site would do about it.

What's Not Working — and Why It Matters

Four specific issues found on your current site, each explained in plain terms.

Critical

Accessibility score of 74 — the lowest category score on your site

What's happening

Google found several accessibility problems: colour contrast is insufficient in places — meaning some text is hard to read — links have no discernible name (likely icon-only links with no text label), and the page has issues with touch targets being too small and missing a main landmark region. Together these push accessibility down to 74 out of 100.

What this means for your patients

A physiotherapy clinic sees patients of all ages, including many who are older or living with conditions that affect vision or mobility. Accessibility issues mean some of those patients are getting a harder experience on your website than they should. It's also a ranking signal — Google increasingly favours accessible sites.

The fix

A rebuild from the ground up with proper contrast ratios, fully labelled links, correctly sized touch targets and a proper semantic page structure. Target: 98+.

Critical

Google can't crawl your links — which is quietly hurting your SEO

What's happening

Google flagged that links are not crawlable — meaning some of your navigation links use JavaScript in a way that search engine crawlers can't follow. This is a common issue with older CMS platforms using custom JS-based navigation. The SEO score is still 92, which is decent, but this is a real risk to your rankings long-term.

What this means for your patients

If Google can't crawl your service pages properly, they may not show up in search results for specific terms like "sports injury physio Eckington" or "acupuncture Sheffield". Given how much effort goes into building a top 3 rated reputation, it's worth making sure the website supports that ranking rather than working against it.

The fix

A cleanly hand-built site uses standard HTML anchor links throughout — fully crawlable by Google from day one. SEO target: 100.

Performance

Render-blocking files adding 1.4 seconds of delay and unused JavaScript slowing things down

What's happening

Google found render-blocking requests adding an estimated 1,440ms of delay — files that must be downloaded before the page can even start showing. There's also 159KB of unused JavaScript being loaded on every visit and 6 long main-thread tasks causing the browser to stall. The site currently scores 84 on performance — decent, but there's clear room to improve.

What this means for your patients

Every second of delay is another moment a potential patient might give up and go elsewhere. You're in a competitive market — the site loading quickly and feeling snappy is part of the first impression.

The fix

A hand-built site with no page builder, no unused plugins, no render-blocking files. Target performance score: 97+.

Best Practices

Missing security headers and low-resolution images flagged by Google

What's happening

Google flagged missing CSP, HSTS, COOP and X-Frame-Options security headers, plus some images being served at lower resolution than expected — meaning they may look soft or blurry on high-resolution screens like modern iPhones. These are pulling your Best Practices score to 88.

What this means for your patients

Low-res images on a clinic website don't reflect the quality of care you provide. Security headers are invisible to patients but expected as standard on any modern professional site.

The fix

All images properly sized and compressed. All security headers configured at deployment. Target: 95+.
