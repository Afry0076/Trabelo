About In The Red

Our Story

Confidence, done properly

I'm Millie Grant, aesthetic practitioner and founder of In The Red Aesthetics, based at the Fitzwilliam Centre in Dalton, Rotherham.

I specialise in enhancing your natural beauty through advanced facial treatments — from anti-wrinkle and fillers to fat dissolving and tattoo laser removal — using the latest techniques and technology. Every treatment starts with an honest conversation, not a sales pitch.

No pressure, no judgement — just personalised care, straightforward advice, and results that still look like you, only more confident.
