Our signature job

The console deep clean that went viral.

Over time, dust, dirt and debris build up inside your console, leading to overheating, performance issues and even hardware failure. Most people clean the fan and stop there — which misses the problem entirely.

The dust builds into "dust carpets" that block the vents on the heat sink. That's what kills the console's ability to disperse heat. The only way to clear it is to fully dismantle the machine — side panels, cooling fan, top shell, disc drive, motherboard, mid-section, right down to the power supply.

  • Every component cleaned, then reassembled and tested
  • Fixes loud fans, random power-offs and overheating shutdowns
  • Also available for laptops, gaming PCs and other electronics
  • Smoother gameplay, faster load times, a longer life out of the machine

Step by step

Inside a console deep clean.

We pride ourselves on providing the best service possible. Here's exactly what happens to your console when you leave it with us — and why cleaning just the fan doesn't work.

01 · Check the fan

Remove the side panel and inspect the cooling fan. Anything more than a light dusting means there's a lot more inside.

02 · Fan & top shell out

Side panels off, then the cooling fan and top shell come away to reveal the inner top side of the console.

03 · Assess the build-up

The top heat sink and disc drive are usually surrounded by dust — and it gives us a first look at the bottom side too.

04 · Disc drive & board

Disc drive out, remaining screws removed, and the main board is ready to lift clear of the chassis.

05 · The dust carpets

Under the mid-section is where dust forms carpets that block the heat sink vents. This is the bit a fan clean never reaches.

06 · Power supply

The PSU vent is another prime spot. It runs warm in normal use — heavy dust build-up here is genuinely a fire risk.

Every part is cleaned, then the console is reassembled and tested. If you think yours might benefit, call us for a quote.

Common questions

Before you bring it in.

Will I lose my data?
Unless your problem is related to software or the motherboard, you should not lose any data during your repair — you wouldn't lose anything during a screen replacement, for example. However, this cannot be guaranteed, as other issues can arise during a repair, so we always recommend backing up your data before bringing it in. We do not wipe or factory reset devices.
What warranty do I get?
We use the best quality parts possible and we back that statement up with a warranty on every repair. Warranties range from 3 months to 12 months. Each repair has a different warranty period, so please ask us for the warranty information relating to your repair.
How do I book a repair?
We operate a walk-in repair service, but if you prefer you can book an appointment. To book a repair, call, email, WhatsApp or message us on Facebook.
How long does a repair take?
It varies by repair type. iPhone screen replacements can be completed within an hour. PC and laptop repairs can be same-day. If your part isn't in stock we may ask for a deposit so you can keep hold of your device while the part is delivered to us — once it arrives, we can complete the repair the same day.
How can I pay?
We currently accept cash or bank transfer.
Where are you, and is there parking?
We're at 205 City Road, Sheffield, S2 5HF — between Granville Road and City Road Post Office, opposite T&G's Sandwich Shop, on the corner of Manor Laith Road. Free parking is available on Manor Laith Road.
Can I send my device by post?
Yes. We accept post-in repairs — this is common for Sat-Navs and Nintendo Switch consoles. Contact us directly to arrange it. Postage is around £5 each way.
Is there anything you don't repair?
We don't repair printers, TVs, monitors, or Sat-Navs built into a vehicle's centre console. Almost everything else, we'll consider.
