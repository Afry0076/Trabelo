About Ellipsis Aesthetics

Two directors. 16 years of NHS experience. Two clinics. One commitment — honest, results-driven aesthetics delivered with genuine care.

Our story

Highly recommended by patients across South Yorkshire

Ellipsis Aesthetics have two modern clinics specialising in non-surgical facial procedures and skin care treatments. Both are led by Natalie Needham and Danielle Talbot — medical practitioners who merged into aesthetics from surgical and intensive care backgrounds within the NHS, bringing 16 years of combined medical experience.

They trained with industry-leading specialists in Manchester and have continued to expand their skills by attending courses, events and conferences across the UK. With 5★ reviews and consistent patient recommendations, Natalie and Danielle have made Ellipsis Aesthetics a highly sought after destination for cosmetic injectables and skin treatments across South Yorkshire.

Co-Director

Natalie Needham

Medical Practitioner & Co-Founder

NHS surgical background — trained with industry-leading specialists in Manchester. Her results-driven approach and 5★ reviews have helped build Ellipsis into South Yorkshire's most recommended aesthetics clinic.

Co-Director

Danielle Talbot

Medical Practitioner & Co-Founder

NHS intensive care background — brings a unique level of clinical precision to her aesthetic work. Continues to develop her skills at UK-wide events, conferences and manufacturer training.

Our Products Promise

Ellipsis offer only the highest quality and market-leading products sourced from UK pharmacies at competitive prices. All products are CE marked, carefully selected and rigorously tested — and only used in our clinics once they've been approved to our high standards.
