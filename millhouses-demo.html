Our story
Plain English accountancy since 2011

Millhouses Accountancy was founded by Jason Scholey in Sheffield in 2011. Since then, the business has grown to operate from offices in Sheffield, Nottingham and London — whilst maintaining the personal, responsive service that characterises everything we do.

We're a Xero certified advisor, a member of the Barnsley & Rotherham Chamber of Commerce, and we're registered with HMRC. All our work is carried out to the highest professional standards.

Whether you're a start-up, a sole trader, a small limited company or an established SME — we have a package or service that works for you. And you'll always deal with the same person, not a different team member every time you call.
