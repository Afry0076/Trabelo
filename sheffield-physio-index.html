Our Story

Award-Winning Care Since 2007

Sheffield Physiotherapy has been providing expert physiotherapy care from our Ecclesall Road clinic since 2007. We've built our reputation on personalised care, the latest technology and outcomes that speak for themselves — winning the Yorkshire Prestige Physiotherapy Clinic of the Year award three consecutive times.

We are the only clinic in the north with some of our specialist equipment — including IDD Therapy spinal decompression and Swiss Piezoclast Focused Shockwave. This investment in technology means our patients have access to treatments that simply aren't available elsewhere in the region.
