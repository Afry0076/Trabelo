About Wigtox

Expert-led aesthetics across South Yorkshire

Wigtox Sculpt & Rejuvenate is a medical aesthetics clinic operating across 11 South Yorkshire locations, led by Registered Nurse Catrionna.

Our mission
Safe, natural, expert-led results

At Wigtox Sculpt & Rejuvenate, we believe aesthetic treatments should be safe, personalised and focused on natural-looking results. We help people feel confident in their own skin — not like a different person.

Our head practitioner Catrionna brings 23 years of clinical experience to every appointment — including A&E and primary care roles, and a Postgraduate Certificate in Advanced Clinical Practice. She is trained in Advanced Life Support, so every treatment is delivered with the medical safety standards that injectable aesthetics demand.

We operate from 11 clinics across South Yorkshire — making professional aesthetic treatments accessible across the region, not just in city centres.
