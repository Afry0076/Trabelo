New Pet Packages

HomeStart — The Right Start for Your New Pet

Getting a new puppy, kitten or rabbit is exciting — and there's a lot to sort out in the first few weeks. Our HomeStart packages cover all the essentials in a single home visit, so you don't have to figure it out yourself.

All HomeStart visits are conducted at your home by Charlotte or Kate. No stressful first journeys, no waiting rooms.

🐶

Puppy HomeStart

First health check, vaccinations start, microchipping, flea & worm advice

🐱

Kitten HomeStart

First health check, vaccines, microchipping, neutering advice and dietary guidance

🐰

Rabbit HomeStart

First health check, vaccination schedule, diet, handling and enrichment guidance

What's Included in HomeStart

  • Full health assessment by a qualified vet
  • Vaccination course started (where appropriate)
  • Microchipping (dogs and cats)
  • Parasite prevention advice and treatment
  • Nutrition and feeding guidance
  • Neutering discussion and planning
  • Insurance options discussion
  • Q&A — no time limit on questions
  • Follow-up call if needed

The Team

Charlotte & Kate

Just the two of us — and that's intentional. You'll always know who's coming and they'll remember your pet.

👩‍⚕️
Veterinary Surgeon

Charlotte

Charlotte is The Travelling Vet's founder and lead vet. She set up the practice to bring professional veterinary care to pets in their own environment — where they're calmer, and where owners are more relaxed too. She has experience across dogs, cats, rabbits and exotics.

👩‍⚕️
Veterinary Nurse

Kate

Kate is the qualified veterinary nurse who assists Charlotte on visits and handles many of the nursing-led consultations. Her calm manner with animals makes her particularly good with anxious or elderly pets who find vet visits stressful at a traditional surgery.
