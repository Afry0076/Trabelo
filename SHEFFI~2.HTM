The headline

Your main content takes 11 seconds to appear on a phone

Google measures something called the Largest Contentful Paint — this is how long it takes before the main content of your page actually shows up on screen for someone on a mobile phone. For Olive Sheffield, that number is 11.1 seconds.

Google's threshold for a "good" result is 2.5 seconds. Anything over 4 seconds is classified as "poor". At 11.1 seconds, someone searching for "Mediterranean restaurant Sheffield" on their phone is looking at a blank or half-loaded screen for more than eleven seconds before they see your restaurant.

11 seconds is a long time to lose a customer

Research from Google shows that more than half of visitors abandon a mobile page if it takes more than 3 seconds to load. At 11 seconds, the vast majority of people searching for somewhere to eat — especially on a Friday or Saturday evening when they're deciding in the moment — will have given up and moved on to somewhere else before your page even loads.

One important note: the FCP (first contentful paint) at 3.3 seconds means something appears reasonably quickly — but the LCP of 11.1 seconds shows that the main content of your page (likely your hero image or banner) takes a very long time to fully load. This creates a frustrating partial-load experience.

Why it's happening

Wix platform overhead and image loading

Your site is built on Wix, which is a flexible tool but one that brings a significant amount of behind-the-scenes code to every page — code that visitors have to download before your site can finish loading. The audit also flagged issues with how your images are being delivered to mobile devices.

132 KiB unused JavaScript 58 KiB image delivery improvement 23 KiB duplicate JavaScript 12 KiB legacy JavaScript 543 internal timing measurements 4+ preconnect connections
  • ⚠️
    132 KiB of unused JavaScript. Your site loads over 130 kilobytes of JavaScript that never actually runs — code for Wix features and platform tools that aren't needed for your pages. On a mobile connection, this takes time to download before anything else can happen.
  • ⚠️
    Image delivery — 58 KiB saving available. Your images aren't being optimally sized and formatted for mobile screens. A mobile visitor is downloading images that are larger than they need to be, which explains part of why the LCP is so high. Properly optimised images would save 58 KiB and significantly speed up the main content load.
  • ⚠️
    Duplicate JavaScript (23 KiB). The same JavaScript code is being loaded twice, adding unnecessary weight to every page load. This is a Wix platform issue rather than something in your own setup.
  • ⚠️
    Legacy JavaScript (12 KiB). There's JavaScript on your site that was written for older browsers and isn't needed for modern devices — but it still has to be downloaded on every visit.
  • ⚠️
    543 internal performance measurements. Wix runs 543 timing and tracking measurements on every page load as part of its own internal monitoring. This adds overhead to every single visit to your website — visible to us in the audit, invisible to you in your dashboard.

None of this can be fixed inside Wix

Every issue listed above is part of how the Wix platform works — they're not settings you can change or things your web designer can tweak inside the Wix editor. The only way to remove this overhead is to move away from a platform-based site to a custom-built one. A custom site starts with none of this baggage: no unused code, no duplicate scripts, no platform tracking, and full control over how images are delivered to mobile.
