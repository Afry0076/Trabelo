Safety & accreditation

Why our accreditations matter to you

In an unregulated industry, SaveFace accreditation and NHS PSA registration are your guarantee of safe, ethical practice.

SaveFace Accredited

SaveFace is the government-approved quality mark for non-surgical cosmetic treatments in the UK. Accreditation means we have been independently assessed to meet high standards of clinical practice, practitioner qualifications, patient safety, complaints handling and clinical governance. It is the gold standard for aesthetic clinics in the UK.

NHS PSA Registered

The NHS Primary Support Agency registration confirms that Enhance Aesthetics meets NHS standards for clinical practice and patient safety — providing an additional layer of oversight and accountability above and beyond what most aesthetic clinics hold.

Medical practitioners only

All injectable treatments at Enhance Aesthetics are administered by qualified medical practitioners — not beauticians or non-medically trained injectors. This matters because complications, though rare, require medical training and emergency medications to manage safely.
