The Founder

Nick Screeton.
Life Enrichment Program.

LEP Fitness isn't just a gym — it's a Life Enrichment Program. Nick founded LEP with a simple belief: that a personal trainer is more than someone who gives you a workout. They're a coach, a friend, and someone who genuinely cares about your quality of life.

With over 10,000 hours of coaching and hundreds of body transformations under his belt, Nick has built a reputation as Sheffield's most trusted personal trainer. Not because of a certificate on a wall — but because his clients get results and keep them long after they stop training with him.

The results you see in the mirror are real. But for Nick, the more important transformation is the one that happens inside — the confidence, the discipline, the resilience that makes everything else in life better too.
