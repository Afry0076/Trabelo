Good to know
How do I pay?

In the salon, after your appointment. Booking online is free and doesn't ask for card details.

I'm new to colour at Jam. What do I need to do?

Book a free colour consultation and skin test first. It's a legal requirement for all new colour clients, and it has to be at least 48 hours before your colour appointment. The online booking sorts this out for you when you choose a colour service.

Why do colour services need a cut or blow dry?

Colour prices don't include a finish, so every colour service needs a blast off, blow dry or cut booked with it. The booking reminds you and adds one with a tap.

Do you offer discounts?

Yes. 15% off for NHS and blue light workers, and 15% off for students. Choose it when you book and bring your ID.

Can I bring my dog?

Yes. Everyone and anyone is welcome, dogs included.

Can I book with Christian?

Do you do perms and extensions?

Yes. Perms start with a free pre-perm consultation, because hair that's been permed before may not be suitable. Extensions (tapes, nanos, micros and weaves) start with a free consultation too.

How do I change or cancel?

Running late?

Tap "Running late" next to your booking on the booking page and the salon sees it straight away.

Privacy

Privacy & cookies

Jam Hairdressing ("we") uses the details you give us (your name, mobile, email and booking notes) only to manage your appointments and reply to your messages. We never sell your details or use them for marketing unless you ask us to.

What we keep

  • Your booking: the services, the time, your stylist and any notes.
  • Your contact details, so we can confirm, remind or change your appointment.
  • Patch-test dates, because the law requires them for colour services.
