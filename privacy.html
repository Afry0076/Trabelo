Privacy Policy

How I handle your information

Last updated: August 2026 · Written in plain English on purpose

I'm Aidan, and I run Trabelo on my own — there's no team, no data-sharing partners, no ad network. Here's exactly what happens to your information when you use this site or get in touch.

What I collect

  • If you use the contact form: your name, business name, email, phone number (if you give it) and your message. The form opens your own email app with that information ready to send to me — it isn't stored on a server anywhere, only in the email you choose to send.
  • If you message me on WhatsApp or email directly: whatever you send me, handled the same way any personal message to me would be — subject to WhatsApp's or your email provider's own privacy policy, not mine.
  • Anonymous site analytics: I use Vercel's privacy-friendly Web Analytics to see roughly how many people visit and which pages get read. It doesn't use cookies and doesn't identify you individually — I only see aggregated numbers, never a profile tied to you.

What I don't do

  • I never sell your details to anyone.
  • I never add you to a mailing list without you asking.
  • I don't use tracking cookies, ad pixels, or third-party trackers on this site.

In short: if you send me a message, I use it to reply to you and, if you go ahead, to build your website. That's it. Nothing gets used or shared for anything else.

How long I keep it

If we end up working together, I keep enquiry and project details for as long as makes sense to support the site and answer any questions — generally no longer than a few years after the work's finished. If nothing comes of an enquiry, I'll delete it within a reasonable time on request.

Your rights

You can ask me what I hold about you, ask me to correct it, or ask me to delete it, at any time — just email me and I'll sort it, usually within a few days.

Questions

If anything here doesn't make sense, or you want something changed or removed, email aidan@trabelo.com — I read and reply to these myself.
