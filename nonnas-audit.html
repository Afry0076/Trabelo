What's Going Wrong

🚫
Your site is hidden from Google
Critical · Kills SEO

What's happening

Your website — every single page of it — has a hidden tag that tells Google: "don't index this." It's called a "noindex" tag, and Wix likely added it at some point, possibly when the site was first being built and was never switched off. Right now, Google's crawler is being actively told not to show your site in search results.

What this means for you

If someone Googles "Italian restaurant Ecclesall Road," "Nonna's Sheffield," or even types your business name directly — your website won't appear. You essentially don't exist on Google at the moment. It's the single biggest issue on the site, and it explains the SEO score of 54.

The fix

On a bespoke site, this is removed entirely. On Wix, it can usually be turned off in the SEO settings — though it's worth checking every page individually, as the tag appears on all of them.

🐌
Your main image takes 11 seconds to appear
Performance · Loses Customers

What's happening

Google measured something called "Largest Contentful Paint" — the time until the main visible content of the page has loaded. For your site, that's 11.0 seconds on a typical mobile connection. That's the number that matters most for whether someone stays on the page or leaves.

What this means for you

Studies consistently show most people give up on a website within 3–4 seconds if nothing loads. At 11 seconds, you're almost certainly losing a significant chunk of potential diners before they've even seen your restaurant. This is partly a Wix limitation — the platform is a general-purpose website builder that loads a lot of code even when it's not needed for your particular page.

The fix

A bespoke build loads only what's needed, images are properly sized and compressed, and there's no platform overhead. A well-built site typically hits 2–3 seconds on mobile even with great imagery.

📦
Over 130KB of code your site doesn't need
Performance · Slow Load

What's happening

Google flagged 113KB of unused JavaScript and 20KB of unused CSS loading on every page. In plain English: your site is downloading a lot of code that it doesn't actually use. This is a common side-effect of Wix — it's a platform built for millions of different websites, so it loads a huge toolkit regardless of whether your specific site needs it.

What this means for you

Every bit of unnecessary code is extra time the visitor's phone has to spend downloading and processing before anything shows on screen. It's one of the main reasons your performance score sits at 71 rather than 90+.

The fix

A custom-built site has no platform overhead — it loads exactly what it needs and nothing else.

🔍
No descriptions showing up in Google results
SEO · Fewer Clicks

What's happening

Your site has no meta descriptions — the short summary text that appears under your listing when you show up in Google search results. When this is missing, Google either shows nothing, or pulls a random sentence from the page that might not sell your restaurant at all.

What this means for you

Even if the noindex issue gets fixed and you do start appearing in search results, people see a bland snippet instead of something like "Sheffield's finest Italian restaurant since 1996 — fresh pasta, live DJ nights and private dining on Ecclesall Road. Book your table." That description difference directly affects whether someone clicks through to you or goes to a competitor.

The fix

A rebuilt site would have a carefully written description for every page, tuned for what people are actually searching for when looking for a restaurant like yours.

🏗️
Built on Wix — a platform with inherent ceilings
Platform Limitation

What's happening

Your site is built on Wix, which is a popular drag-and-drop website builder. It's a perfectly reasonable way to get a site up quickly, but it has real performance and flexibility ceilings — some of which we've already seen above.

What this means for you

Even with the noindex and SEO issues fixed, Wix sites are fundamentally heavier than bespoke ones. You're also paying a monthly Wix subscription for a platform that's actively holding your scores back. A custom-built site is yours outright — no monthly platform fee, no limitations, and you're not sharing infrastructure with millions of other sites.

The fix

A proper bespoke rebuild. Lightweight, fast, fully owned, and designed specifically for Nonna's — not a template adapted for you.
