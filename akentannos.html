Who we are

Our Story

Akentannos is Sheffield's first and only Sardinian restaurant — family-run by Mario and Giovanna, and situated on the vibrant Sharrow Vale Road.

Our philosophy fits perfectly the criteria of those who love this land of sun and blue sea, natural fragrances and intense flavours, where the soul of Sardinian people gives life to every single dish.

We pride ourselves in using the finest and freshest ingredients, imported in majority from Sardinia — and wherever possible, locally sourced from South Yorkshire suppliers. Everything is prepared on the spot, respecting the right cooking times, to enhance the true aromas and flavours of the island.

We also have a strong reputation for gluten free dishes, and have developed a full vegan menu alongside our main menu.

About the name

A Kent'Annos

"May you live one hundred years"

Akentannos takes its name from a traditional Sardinian greeting — A Kent'Annos — a toast to long life and good health. It's not an idle wish: Sardinians are among the longest-lived people on earth.

Sardinia is one of the world's original "Blue Zones" — places where people consistently live well past 100. Scientists have spent decades studying what makes Sardinians so resilient. The answer, it turns out, lies partly in what they eat and drink: Cannonau wine, Pecorino Sardo cheese, wild-foraged vegetables, and a diet built on simplicity and quality.

That same philosophy drives everything at Akentannos — eat well, eat real, and eat together.
