Why Whirlow Brook Hall
Charm, tradition and modern elegance — all in one.

Set in 39 stunning acres with beautiful views across Sheffield — Whirlow Brook Hall is a venue that feels genuinely special in every season.

01
Exclusive hire — the whole venue is yours
We follow a strict exclusive booking policy. When you book Whirlow Brook Hall, you have full access to the entire venue and grounds for your event — no other parties, no interruptions.
02
4 miles from Sheffield city centre
Close enough for your guests to get to easily — far enough to feel like a real escape. Located near the Peak District and Chesterfield with excellent road links.
