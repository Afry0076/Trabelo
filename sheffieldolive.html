Who we are

Freya & Rachid's Story

Olive is a unique dining experience, established in 2018 and independently owned and run by Freya and Rachid, whose love of hospitality is evident in everything about the food and service.

Chef Rachid brings a rare combination of influences to the kitchen — his Moroccan roots and time spent living and working in Italian kitchens in Emilia-Romagna, north Italy. The result is a menu that you genuinely won't find anywhere else in Sheffield: North African spice meets Italian craft, all made fresh each evening.

Olive has gone through changes since 2018 — navigating Covid, the financial crisis, and widespread staff shortages in the hospitality sector — and has kept adapting to the wants and needs of its regulars while striving to remain a genuinely great restaurant. In late 2023, the opportunity to take over the shop next door arose, and Freya and Rachid couldn't say no.

The result is the Olive you see today: bigger, more comfortable, and with the same independently-run spirit at its heart.
