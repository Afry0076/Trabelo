Types of claim

What kind of accident can you claim for?

If the accident wasn't your fault and you were injured, there's a good chance you have a claim. Here are some of the most common types we handle.

🚗

Road Traffic Accidents

Hit by another driver, a cyclist, or injured as a passenger? Road traffic accidents are one of the most common personal injury claims — and one of the most straightforward to pursue.

  • Car accidents
  • Motorcycle injuries
  • Cyclist & pedestrian claims
🏭

Workplace Injuries

Your employer has a legal duty to keep you safe at work. If they've failed in that duty and you've been hurt, you're entitled to claim — and you can't be dismissed for doing so.

  • Slips & falls at work
  • Manual handling injuries
  • Machinery & equipment accidents
🚶

Public Place Accidents

Slipped on a wet floor in a shop? Tripped on a broken pavement? If a business or council failed to maintain a safe environment, you have a right to claim.

  • Slips & trips in shops
  • Pavement & pothole injuries
  • Accidents in public buildings

What counts as clinical negligence

When a medical professional lets you down.

Clinical negligence occurs when a healthcare professional provides treatment that falls below the accepted standard of care, and that failure causes you harm. It's not about bad outcomes — it's about whether the care you received was adequate.

🔬

Misdiagnosis & Delayed Diagnosis

A missed or delayed diagnosis can have serious consequences — especially for conditions like cancer, heart disease or stroke where early treatment is critical.

  • Cancer misdiagnosis
  • Delayed diagnosis
  • Incorrect diagnosis
🏥

Surgical Errors

Errors during surgery — operating on the wrong site, leaving instruments inside a patient, or causing unnecessary damage — are serious failures that you're entitled to pursue.

  • Wrong-site surgery
  • Anaesthesia errors
  • Post-operative negligence
💊

Medication Errors

Being given the wrong medication, the wrong dose, or a drug you're allergic to is a serious failure. If a medication error caused you harm, you have a right to claim.

  • Wrong medication
  • Incorrect dosage
  • Failure to check allergies
